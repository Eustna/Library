# 第 4 章 量子旋量场

本章讨论旋量场 (spinor field) 的正则量子化，旋量场对应于旋量表示 (spinor representation)。旋量表示是固有保时向 Lorentz 群 $\mathrm{SO}^\uparrow(1,3)$ 的一个投影表示，也是相应覆盖群 $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ 的一个线性表示。Paul Dirac 在 1928 年首次将旋量表示引入到描述电子的理论中，建立了 Dirac 方程 [3]。$\mathrm{SO}^\uparrow(1,3)$ 和 $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ 的 Lie 代数都是 Lorentz 代数，它们的表示可以通过构造满足 Lorentz 代数关系 (3.31) 的生成元矩阵来得到，下面我们以这样的方式建立旋量表示。

## 4.1 Lorentz 群的旋量表示

首先，假设能够找到一组满足如下反对易关系的 $N \times N$ 矩阵 $\gamma^\mu$ ($\mu = 0, 1, 2, 3$)：

$$
\{\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu\} \equiv \gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu + \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu = 2g^{\mu\nu}\mathbf{1} = 2g^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{4.1}
$$

最后一步是一种简写，省略了 $N \times N$ 单位矩阵 $\mathbf{1}$。这样的 $\gamma^\mu$ 称为 Dirac 矩阵，也称为 $\gamma$ 矩阵。这个反对易关系意味着

$$
\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu = 2g^{\mu\nu} - \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu.
\tag{4.2}
$$

当 $\mu \neq \nu$ 时，$g^{\mu\nu} = 0$，而 $\gamma^\mu$ 与 $\gamma^\nu$ 是反对易的，即

$$
\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu = -\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu, \quad \mu \neq \nu.
\tag{4.3}
$$

当 $\mu = \nu$ 时，有

$$
(\gamma^0)^2 = \frac{1}{2}\{\gamma^0, \gamma^0\} = g^{00} = \mathbf{1}, \quad (\gamma^i)^2 = \frac{1}{2}\{\gamma^i, \gamma^i\} = g^{ii} = -\mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.4}
$$

因此，$(\gamma^0)^2$ 的本征值都是 1，$(\gamma^i)^2$ 的本征值都是 $-1$。这意味着 $\gamma^0$ 的本征值为实数 $\pm 1$，$\gamma^i$ 的本征值为虚数 $\pm\mathrm{i}$，将它们对角化，则对角元是这些本征值。这些性质告诉我们，$\gamma^0$ 是厄米矩阵，$\gamma^i$ 是反厄米矩阵，即

$$
(\gamma^0)^\dagger = \gamma^0, \quad (\gamma^i)^\dagger = -\gamma^i.
\tag{4.5}
$$

于是

$$
(\gamma^0)^\dagger\gamma^0 = (\gamma^0)^2 = \mathbf{1}, \quad (\gamma^i)^\dagger\gamma^i = -(\gamma^i)^2 = \mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.6}
$$

可见 $\gamma^0$ 和 $\gamma^i$ 都是幺正矩阵。

然后，以 Dirac 矩阵的对易子定义另一组 $N \times N$ 矩阵

$$
S^{\mu\nu} \equiv \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu].
\tag{4.7}
$$

显然，$S^{\mu\nu}$ 关于 $\mu$ 和 $\nu$ 反对称，

$$
S^{\mu\nu} = -S^{\nu\mu}.
\tag{4.8}
$$

因而 $S^{\mu\nu}$ 的独立分量有 6 个。

利用对易子公式

$$
[AB, C] = ABC + ACB - ACB - CAB = A\{B, C\} - \{A, C\}B,
\tag{4.9}
$$

推出

$$
\begin{aligned}
[S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\rho] &= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu - \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\rho] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu - (2g^{\mu\nu} - \gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu), \gamma^\rho] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu, \gamma^\rho] \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}(\gamma^\mu\{\gamma^\nu, \gamma^\rho\} - \{\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\rho\}\gamma^\nu) = \mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\rho} - \gamma^\nu g^{\mu\rho}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.10}
$$

从而，根据对易子公式 (2.13) 有

$$
\begin{aligned}
[S^{\mu\nu}, S^{\rho\sigma}] &= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma - \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\rho] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}([S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma] - [S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\rho]) \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}([S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\rho]\gamma^\sigma + \gamma^\rho[S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\sigma] - [S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\sigma]\gamma^\rho - \gamma^\sigma[S^{\mu\nu}, \gamma^\rho]) \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\rho} - \gamma^\nu g^{\mu\rho})\gamma^\sigma + \mathrm{i}\gamma^\rho(\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\sigma} - \gamma^\nu g^{\mu\sigma}) \\
&\quad - \mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\sigma} - \gamma^\nu g^{\mu\sigma})\gamma^\rho - \mathrm{i}\gamma^\sigma(\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\rho} - \gamma^\nu g^{\mu\rho})] \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}^2}{4}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\sigma g^{\nu\rho} - \gamma^\nu\gamma^\sigma g^{\mu\rho} + \gamma^\rho\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\sigma} - \gamma^\rho\gamma^\nu g^{\mu\sigma} \\
&\quad - \gamma^\mu\gamma^\rho g^{\nu\sigma} + \gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho g^{\mu\sigma} - \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\mu g^{\nu\rho} + \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\nu g^{\mu\rho}) \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}^2}{4}[g^{\nu\rho}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\sigma - \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\mu) - g^{\mu\rho}(\gamma^\nu\gamma^\sigma - \gamma^\sigma\gamma^\nu) - g^{\nu\sigma}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\rho - \gamma^\rho\gamma^\mu) + g^{\mu\sigma}(\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho - \gamma^\rho\gamma^\nu)] \\
&= \mathrm{i}(g^{\nu\rho}S^{\mu\sigma} - g^{\mu\rho}S^{\nu\sigma} - g^{\nu\sigma}S^{\mu\rho} + g^{\mu\sigma}S^{\nu\rho}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.11}
$$

可见，$S^{\mu\nu}$ 满足 Lorentz 代数关系 (3.31)，因而是 Lorentz 群某个表示的生成元。以 $S^{\mu\nu}$ 生成的表示就是旋量表示。

根据 3.2 节的讨论，一组变换参数 $\omega_{\mu\nu}$ 在 Lorentz 群的矢量表示中生成固有保时向的有限变换 (3.153)：

$$
\Lambda = \exp\left(-\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}\mathcal{J}^{\mu\nu}\right) = \mathrm{e}^X, \quad X \equiv -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}\mathcal{J}^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{4.12}
$$

类似地，这组参数在旋量表示中生成固有保时向的有限变换

$$
D(\Lambda) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}\left(-\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right)^n = \exp\left(-\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right) = \mathrm{e}^Y, \quad Y \equiv -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{4.13}
$$

这样定义的 $D(\Lambda)$ 是旋量表示中的 Lorentz 变换矩阵。由 (3.72) 式有

$$
\mathrm{e}^{-Y}\mathrm{e}^Y = \mathrm{e}^{-Y+Y} = \mathrm{e}^0 = \mathbf{1},
\tag{4.14}
$$

故 $D(\Lambda)$ 的逆矩阵为

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda) = \mathrm{e}^{-Y} = \exp\left(\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right).
\tag{4.15}
$$

这里介绍一些将会用到的对易子公式。对于算符或同阶方阵 $B$ 与 $A$，以如下方式定义它们的多重对易子 $[B, A^{(n)}]$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&[B, A^{(0)}] \equiv B, \quad [B, A^{(1)}] \equiv [[B, A^{(0)}], A] = [B, A] \\
&[B, A^{(2)}] \equiv [[B, A^{(1)}], A] = [[B, A], A], \quad \cdots, \quad [B, A^{(n)}] \equiv [[B, A^{(n-1)}], A].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.16}
$$

从而

$$
BA^k = \sum_{n=0}^k\frac{k!}{(k-n)!n!}A^{k-n}[B, A^{(n)}].
\tag{4.17}
$$

下面用数学归纳法证明这个等式。

证明　当 $k = 0$ 和 $k = 1$ 时，(4.17) 式明显成立：

$$
BA^0 = B = [B, A^{(0)}] = \frac{0!}{(0-0)!0!}A^{0-0}[B, A^{(0)}],
\tag{4.18}
$$

$$
BA^1 = BA = AB + [B, A] = \frac{1!}{(1-0)!0!}A^{1-0}[B, A^{(0)}] + \frac{1!}{(1-1)!1!}A^{1-1}[B, A^{(1)}].
\tag{4.19}
$$

假设 $k = m$ 时 (4.17) 式成立，则

$$
\begin{aligned}
BA^{m+1} &= \sum_{n=0}^m\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}A^{m-n}[B, A^{(n)}]A = \sum_{n=0}^m\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}A^{m-n}(A[B, A^{(n)}] + [[B, A^{(n)}], A]) \\
&= \sum_{n=0}^m\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}A^{m+1-n}[B, A^{(n)}] + \sum_{n=0}^m\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}A^{m-n}[B, A^{(n+1)}] \\
&= \sum_{n=0}^m\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}A^{m+1-n}[B, A^{(n)}] + \sum_{j=1}^{m+1}\frac{m!}{(m-j+1)!(j-1)!}A^{m-j+1}[B, A^{(j)}] \\
&= \frac{m!}{(m-0)!0!}A^{m+1}[B, A^{(0)}] + \sum_{n=1}^m\left[\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!} + \frac{m!}{(m-n+1)!(n-1)!}\right]A^{m+1-n}[B, A^{(n)}] \\
&\quad + \frac{m!}{[m-(m+1)+1]![(m+1)-1]!}A^{m-(m+1)+1}[B, A^{(m+1)}] \\
&= A^{m+1}[B, A^{(0)}] + \sum_{n=1}^m\left[\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!} + \frac{n}{m-n+1}\frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}\right]A^{m+1-n}[B, A^{(n)}] \\
&\quad + A^{m-(m+1)+1}[B, A^{(m+1)}] \\
&= \frac{(m+1)!}{[(m+1)-0]!0!}A^{m+1}[B, A^{(0)}] + \sum_{n=1}^m\frac{(m+1)!}{(m-n+1)!n!}A^{m+1-n}[B, A^{(n)}] \\
&\quad + \frac{(m+1)!}{[(m+1)-(m+1)]!(m+1)!}A^{m-(m+1)+1}[B, A^{(m+1)}] \\
&= \sum_{n=0}^{m+1}\frac{(m+1)!}{[(m+1)-n]!n!}A^{(m+1)-n}[B, A^{(n)}],
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.20}
$$

即 $k = m + 1$ 时 (4.17) 式也成立。于是，(4.17) 式对任意非负整数 $k$ 成立。证毕。

根据推广的阶乘定义 (3.69) 将 (4.17) 式右边的级数化成无穷级数，

$$
BA^k = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{k!}{(k-n)!n!}A^{k-n}[B, A^{(n)}].
\tag{4.21}
$$

利用上式推出

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{e}^{-A}B\mathrm{e}^A &= \mathrm{e}^{-A}\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{1}{k!}BA^k = \mathrm{e}^{-A}\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{k!}{(k-n)!n!}A^{k-n}[B, A^{(n)}] \\
&= \mathrm{e}^{-A}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{1}{(k-n)!}A^{k-n}[B, A^{(n)}] = \mathrm{e}^{-A}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}\mathrm{e}^A[B, A^{(n)}],
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.22}
$$

即

$$
\mathrm{e}^{-A}B\mathrm{e}^A = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}[B, A^{(n)}].
\tag{4.23}
$$

现在，我们继续讨论 Lorentz 群的旋量表示。由 (4.10) 和 (3.141) 式得

$$
[\gamma^\mu, S^{\rho\sigma}] = -[S^{\rho\sigma}, \gamma^\mu] = [S^{\sigma\rho}, \gamma^\mu] = \mathrm{i}(\gamma^\sigma g^{\rho\mu} - \gamma^\rho g^{\sigma\mu}) = \mathrm{i}(g^{\rho\mu}\delta^\sigma{}_\nu - g^{\sigma\mu}\delta^\rho{}_\nu)\gamma^\nu = (\mathcal{J}^{\rho\sigma})^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu.
\tag{4.24}
$$

从而，

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\gamma^\mu, Y^{(1)}] &= [\gamma^\mu, Y] = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\rho\sigma}[\gamma^\mu, S^{\rho\sigma}] = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\rho\sigma}(\mathcal{J}^{\rho\sigma})^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu = X^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu, \\
[\gamma^\mu, Y^{(2)}] &= [[\gamma^\mu, Y^{(1)}], Y] = X^\mu{}_\nu[\gamma^\nu, Y] = X^\mu{}_\nu X^\nu{}_\rho\gamma^\rho = (X^2)^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu, \\
&\cdots \\
[\gamma^\mu, Y^{(n)}] &= (X^n)^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.25}
$$

再利用 (4.23) 式推出

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda) = \mathrm{e}^{-Y}\gamma^\mu\mathrm{e}^Y = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}[\gamma^\mu, Y^{(n)}] = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}(X^n)^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu = (\mathrm{e}^X)^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu,
\tag{4.26}
$$

即

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu.
\tag{4.27}
$$

类比 (3.24) 式，把上式看作旋量表示中 $\gamma^\mu$ 矩阵的 Lorentz 变换规则，那么 $\gamma^\mu$ 是一个 Lorentz 矢量。相应的协变矢量为

$$
\gamma_\mu \equiv g_{\mu\nu}\gamma^\nu,
\tag{4.28}
$$

从而

$$
\gamma_0 = \gamma^0, \quad \gamma_i = -\gamma^i, \quad i = 1, 2, 3.
\tag{4.29}
$$

$N \times N$ 单位矩阵 $\mathbf{1}$ 满足

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\mathbf{1}D(\Lambda) = \mathbf{1},
\tag{4.30}
$$

因而 $\mathbf{1}$ 是一个 Lorentz 标量。生成元 $S^{\mu\nu}$ 的 Lorentz 变换形式为

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)S^{\mu\nu}D(\Lambda) = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda), D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\nu D(\Lambda)] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}\Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\Lambda^\nu{}_\sigma[\gamma^\rho, \gamma^\sigma] = \Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\Lambda^\nu{}_\sigma S^{\rho\sigma},
\tag{4.31}
$$

可见，$S^{\mu\nu}$ 是一个 2 阶反对称 Lorentz 张量。

引入一个新的矩阵

$$
\gamma^5 \equiv \gamma_5 \equiv \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3.
\tag{4.32}
$$

根据 (4.3) 式，有

$$
\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma = \begin{cases}
+\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3, & (\mu, \nu, \rho, \sigma)\ \text{是}\ (0, 1, 2, 3)\ \text{的偶置换}, \\
-\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3, & (\mu, \nu, \rho, \sigma)\ \text{是}\ (0, 1, 2, 3)\ \text{的奇置换}.
\end{cases}
\tag{4.33}
$$

这种置换性质与四维 Levi-Civita 符号 (1.83) 相同，因而置换操作带来的符号在 $\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ 与 $\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma$ 的乘积中相互抵消，如

$$
\varepsilon_{1023}\gamma^1\gamma^0\gamma^2\gamma^3 = -\varepsilon_{0123}(-\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3) = \varepsilon_{0123}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3.
\tag{4.34}
$$

由此得到

$$
\gamma^5 = \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3 = -\mathrm{i}\varepsilon_{0123}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3 = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4!}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma.
\tag{4.35}
$$

$\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ 是 $(0, 4)$ 型 Lorentz 张量，固有 Lorentz 变换形式为

$$
\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} = \varepsilon_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}(\Lambda^{-1})^\alpha{}_\mu(\Lambda^{-1})^\beta{}_\nu(\Lambda^{-1})^\gamma{}_\rho(\Lambda^{-1})^\delta{}_\sigma.
\tag{4.36}
$$

从而，对于固有保时向 Lorentz 变换 (4.12)，$\gamma^5$ 的变换形式是

$$
\begin{aligned}
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^5 D(\Lambda) &= -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4!}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\Lambda^\mu{}_\alpha\Lambda^\nu{}_\beta\Lambda^\rho{}_\gamma\Lambda^\sigma{}_\delta\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\beta\gamma^\gamma\gamma^\delta \\
&= -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4!}\varepsilon_{\kappa\lambda\tau\varepsilon}(\Lambda^{-1})^\kappa{}_\mu(\Lambda^{-1})^\lambda{}_\nu(\Lambda^{-1})^\tau{}_\rho(\Lambda^{-1})^\varepsilon{}_\sigma\Lambda^\mu{}_\alpha\Lambda^\nu{}_\beta\Lambda^\rho{}_\gamma\Lambda^\sigma{}_\delta\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\beta\gamma^\gamma\gamma^\delta \\
&= -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4!}\varepsilon_{\kappa\lambda\tau\varepsilon}\delta^\kappa{}_\alpha\delta^\lambda{}_\beta\delta^\tau{}_\gamma\delta^\varepsilon{}_\delta\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\beta\gamma^\gamma\gamma^\delta = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4!}\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\beta\gamma^\gamma\gamma^\delta = \gamma^5.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.37}
$$

可见，$\gamma^5$ 是一个 Lorentz 标量。$\gamma^5$ 的平方为

$$
(\gamma^5)^2 = -\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3 = -\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\gamma^3\gamma^2\gamma^1\gamma^0 = -(-\mathbf{1})^3 = \mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.38}
$$

第二步利用偶置换将后面的 $\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$ 转化为 $\gamma^3\gamma^2\gamma^1\gamma^0$。根据 (4.5) 式，$\gamma^5$ 是厄米矩阵，

$$
(\gamma^5)^\dagger = -\mathrm{i}(\gamma^3)^\dagger(\gamma^2)^\dagger(\gamma^1)^\dagger(\gamma^0)^\dagger = \mathrm{i}\gamma^3\gamma^2\gamma^1\gamma^0 = \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3 = \gamma^5.
\tag{4.39}
$$

此外，$\gamma^5$ 与 $\gamma^\mu$ 反对易，

$$
\{\gamma^5, \gamma^\mu\} = \mathrm{i}(\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\gamma^\mu + \gamma^\mu\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3) = \mathrm{i}(\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\gamma^\mu - \gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\gamma^\mu) = 0,
\tag{4.40}
$$

即

$$
\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 = -\gamma^5\gamma^\mu.
\tag{4.41}
$$

$\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$ 的 Lorentz 变换形式为

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 D(\Lambda) = D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^5 D(\Lambda) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu\gamma^5,
\tag{4.42}
$$

因而它是一个 Lorentz 矢量。再引入

$$
\sigma^{\mu\nu} \equiv \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}[\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu] = 2S^{\mu\nu},
\tag{4.43}
$$

它正比于 $S^{\mu\nu}$，所以也是一个 2 阶反对称 Lorentz 张量：

$$
D^{-1}(\Lambda)\sigma^{\mu\nu}D(\Lambda) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\Lambda^\nu{}_\sigma\sigma^{\rho\sigma}.
\tag{4.44}
$$

根据 (4.3) 式，当 $\mu \neq \nu$ 时，$[\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu] = \gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu - \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu = 2\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu$，故 $\sigma^{\mu\nu}$ 的 6 个独立分量为

$$
\sigma^{01} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^1, \quad \sigma^{02} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^2, \quad \sigma^{03} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^3, \quad \sigma^{12} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^1\gamma^2, \quad \sigma^{13} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^1\gamma^3, \quad \sigma^{23} = \mathrm{i}\gamma^2\gamma^3.
\tag{4.45}
$$

现在，我们拥有一组矩阵

$$
\{\mathbf{1}, \gamma^5, \gamma^\mu, \gamma^\mu\gamma^5, \sigma^{\mu\nu}\},
\tag{4.46}
$$

它们各自的独立分量个数之和为 $1 + 1 + 4 + 4 + 6 = 16$。上面已经讨论了这些矩阵的固有保时向 Lorentz 变换性质，下面研究它们的宇称变换性质。

用 $\gamma^0$ 定义变换矩阵

$$
D(\mathcal{P}) = \zeta\gamma^0,
\tag{4.47}
$$

其中 $\zeta$ 是一个复的相位因子，满足 $|\zeta|^2 = 1$。$D^\dagger(\mathcal{P})D(\mathcal{P}) = \zeta^*(\gamma^0)^\dagger\zeta\gamma^0 = |\zeta|^2(\gamma^0)^2 = \mathbf{1}$ 表明，$D(\mathcal{P})$ 是幺正矩阵，故

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P}) = D^\dagger(\mathcal{P}) = \zeta^*\gamma^0.
\tag{4.48}
$$

用 $D(\mathcal{P})$ 对 $\gamma^0$ 和 $\gamma^i$ 作相似变换，得

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^0 D(\mathcal{P}) = |\zeta|^2\gamma^0\gamma^0\gamma^0 = +\gamma^0, \quad D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^i D(\mathcal{P}) = |\zeta|^2\gamma^0\gamma^i\gamma^0 = -\gamma^i\gamma^0\gamma^0 = -\gamma^i.
\tag{4.49}
$$

利用宇称变换 $\mathcal{P}$ 的定义 (1.57)，将这两个式子归纳为

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^\mu D(\mathcal{P}) = \mathcal{P}^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu.
\tag{4.50}
$$

可见，$D(\mathcal{P})$ 就是旋量表示中的宇称变换矩阵，它是非固有保时向的。上式是 $\gamma^\mu$ 的宇称变换形式，按照 1.4 节的定义，$\gamma^\mu$ 是极矢量。(4.49) 式说明 $\gamma^0$ 是宇称本征态，本征值为 $+$，即具有偶宇称；$\gamma^i$ 也是宇称本征态，本征值为 $-$，即具有奇宇称。

虽然单位矩阵 $\mathbf{1}$ 与 $\gamma^5$ 都是 Lorentz 标量，但它们的宇称变换性质不同，

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\mathbf{1}D(\mathcal{P}) = +\mathbf{1}, \quad D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^5 D(\mathcal{P}) = \gamma^0\gamma^5\gamma^0 = -\gamma^5\gamma^0\gamma^0 = -\gamma^5.
\tag{4.51}
$$

根据 1.5 节的定义，$\mathbf{1}$ 是狭义的标量，具有偶宇称；$\gamma^5$ 是赝标量，具有奇宇称。此外，$\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$ 的宇称变换形式是

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 D(\mathcal{P}) = D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^\mu D(\mathcal{P})D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^5 D(\mathcal{P}) = -\mathcal{P}^\mu{}_\nu\gamma^\nu\gamma^5,
\tag{4.52}
$$

即

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^0\gamma^5 D(\mathcal{P}) = -\gamma^0\gamma^5, \quad D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^i\gamma^5 D(\mathcal{P}) = +\gamma^i\gamma^5.
\tag{4.53}
$$

根据 1.4 节的定义，$\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$ 是轴矢量，其分量的宇称性质与 $\gamma^\mu$ 相反。最后，$\sigma^{\mu\nu}$ 的宇称变换形式为

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\sigma^{\mu\nu}D(\mathcal{P}) = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}[D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^\mu D(\mathcal{P}), D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\gamma^\nu D(\mathcal{P})] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\mathcal{P}^\mu{}_\alpha\mathcal{P}^\nu{}_\beta[\gamma^\alpha, \gamma^\beta] = \mathcal{P}^\mu{}_\alpha\mathcal{P}^\nu{}_\beta\sigma^{\alpha\beta},
\tag{4.54}
$$

即

$$
D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\sigma^{0i}D(\mathcal{P}) = \mathcal{P}^0{}_\alpha\mathcal{P}^i{}_\beta\sigma^{\alpha\beta} = -\sigma^{0i}, \quad D^{-1}(\mathcal{P})\sigma^{ij}D(\mathcal{P}) = \mathcal{P}^i{}_\alpha\mathcal{P}^j{}_\beta\sigma^{\alpha\beta} = +\sigma^{ij}.
\tag{4.55}
$$

可见，集合 (4.46) 是由标量 $\mathbf{1}$、赝标量 $\gamma^5$、极矢量 $\gamma^\mu$、轴矢量 $\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$ 和 2 阶反对称张量 $\sigma^{\mu\nu}$ 组成的。综合考虑固有保时向 Lorentz 变换和宇称变换，则这些矩阵的变换性质各不相同，因而彼此之间是线性独立的，总共有 16 个线性独立的矩阵。由于线性独立的 $N \times N$ 矩阵至多有 $N^2$ 个，需要 $N \geq 4$ 才能得到 16 个这样的矩阵。取 $N = 4$，就可以用这 16 个矩阵展开一个任意的 $4 \times 4$ 矩阵，也就是说，它们构成一组完备的基底。因此，将 Dirac 矩阵 $\gamma^\mu$ 取为 $4 \times 4$ 矩阵，而旋量表示是 Lorentz 群的一个 4 维表示。

## 4.2 Dirac 旋量场

在 Lorentz 群的旋量表示中，被变换矩阵 $D(\Lambda)$ 作用的态称为 Dirac 旋量 (spinor)。由于 $D(\Lambda)$ 是 $4 \times 4$ 矩阵，一个 Dirac 旋量 $\psi_a$ 应当具有 4 个分量 ($a = 1, 2, 3, 4$)，相应的固有保时向 Lorentz 变换形式为

$$
\psi'_a = D_{ab}(\Lambda)\psi_b.
\tag{4.56}
$$

隐去旋量指标 $a$ 和 $b$，上式化为

$$
\psi' = D(\Lambda)\psi.
\tag{4.57}
$$

我们可以将 $\psi$ 和 $\psi'$ 看作列矢量，而上式右边的乘积就是线性代数中矩阵与列矢量的乘积。

进一步，如果 $\psi_a$ 依赖于时空坐标 $x^\mu$，它就成为 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi_a(x)$。类似于 (3.169) 式，量子 Dirac 旋量场的 Lorentz 变换形式是

$$
\psi'_a(x') = U^{-1}(\Lambda)\psi_a(x')U(\Lambda) = D_{ab}(\Lambda)\psi_b(x).
\tag{4.58}
$$

对于固有保时向 Lorentz 变换，由 (4.13) 式得到 $D_{ab}(\Lambda)$ 的无穷小形式为

$$
D_{ab}(\Lambda) = \delta_{ab} - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})_{ab},
\tag{4.59}
$$

于是，(4.58) 式的无穷小形式是

$$
\psi'_a(x') = \psi_a(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})_{ab}\psi_b(x).
\tag{4.60}
$$

将上式与 (1.203) 式比较，可以发现 1.7.3 小节中的生成元 $J^{\mu\nu}$ 在旋量表示中对应于 $S^{\mu\nu}$。隐去旋量指标，则 (4.58) 式化为

$$
\psi'(x') = U^{-1}(\Lambda)\psi(x')U(\Lambda) = D(\Lambda)\psi(x),
\tag{4.61}
$$

也可以写成

$$
U^{-1}(\Lambda)\psi(x)U(\Lambda) = D(\Lambda)\psi(\Lambda^{-1}x).
\tag{4.62}
$$

对于无穷小变换，根据 (3.161) 式，在 $x$ 处将 $\psi(\Lambda^{-1}x)$ 展开到 $\omega$ 的一阶项，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi(\Lambda^{-1}x) &= \psi(x) - \omega^\mu{}_\nu x^\nu\partial_\mu\psi(x) = \psi(x) - \omega_{\mu\nu}x^\nu\partial^\mu\psi(x) \\
&= \psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}\mathrm{i}(x^\mu\partial^\nu - x^\nu\partial^\mu)\psi(x) = \psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}L^{\mu\nu}\psi(x),
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.63}
$$

其中 $L^{\mu\nu}$ 是 (3.163) 式定义的微分算符。从而，(4.62) 式右边展开到 $\omega$ 一阶项的形式为

$$
D(\Lambda)\psi(\Lambda^{-1}x) = \left(\mathbf{1} - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right)\left[\psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}L^{\mu\nu}\psi(x)\right] = \psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(L^{\mu\nu} + S^{\mu\nu})\psi(x).
\tag{4.64}
$$

另一方面，根据 (3.10) 式将 (4.62) 式左边展开为

$$
\begin{aligned}
U^{-1}(\Lambda)\psi(x)U(\Lambda) &= \left(1 + \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\rho\sigma}J^{\rho\sigma}\right)\psi(x)\left(1 - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}J^{\mu\nu}\right) \\
&= \psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}\psi(x)J^{\mu\nu} + \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\rho\sigma}J^{\rho\sigma}\psi(x) = \psi(x) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}[\psi(x), J^{\mu\nu}].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.65}
$$

两相比较，得到

$$
[\psi(x), J^{\mu\nu}] = (L^{\mu\nu} + S^{\mu\nu})\psi(x).
\tag{4.66}
$$

$S^{\mu\nu}$ 纯空间分量的对偶三维矢量为

$$
S^i \equiv \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon^{ijk}S^{jk}, \quad \mathbf{S} = (S^{23}, S^{31}, S^{12}).
\tag{4.67}
$$

可以从 Lorentz 代数关系 (4.11) 推出类似于 (3.39) 式的对易关系 $[S^i, S^j] = \mathrm{i}\varepsilon^{ijk}S^k$，因而 $S^i$ 是 SU(2) 群某个线性表示的生成元。再根据 (3.35) 和 (3.166) 式，将 (4.66) 式的纯空间分量改写为

$$
[\psi(x), \mathbf{J}] = (\mathbf{L} + \mathbf{S})\psi(x).
\tag{4.68}
$$

上式表明，除了轨道角动量 $\mathbf{L}$，总角动量算符 $\mathbf{J}$ 还生成了由 $\mathbf{S}$ 描述的自旋角动量。

利用 Pauli 矩阵 (3.46)，可以将 Dirac 矩阵表示成 $2 \times 2$ 分块形式：

$$
\gamma^0 = \begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix}, \quad \gamma^i = \begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^i \\ -\sigma^i & \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.69}
$$

其中 $\mathbf{1}$ 表示 $2 \times 2$ 单位矩阵。容易验证，这样表示的 Dirac 矩阵符合 (4.5) 式，而且满足反对易关系 (4.1)：

$$
\{\gamma^0, \gamma^0\} = 2\begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix} = 2\begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1} & \\ & \mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix} = 2g^{00},
\tag{4.70}
$$

$$
\{\gamma^0, \gamma^i\} = \begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^i & \\ & \sigma^i \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} \sigma^i & \\ & -\sigma^i \end{pmatrix} = 0 = 2g^{0i},
\tag{4.71}
$$

$$
\{\gamma^i, \gamma^j\} = \begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^i\sigma^j - \sigma^j\sigma^i & \\ & -\sigma^i\sigma^j - \sigma^j\sigma^i \end{pmatrix} = -2\delta^{ij}\begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1} & \\ & \mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix} = 2g^{ij}.
\tag{4.72}
$$

实际上，Dirac 矩阵有多种表示方式，(4.69) 式这种表示方式称为 Weyl 表象，也称为手征表象 (chiral representation)。Dirac 矩阵的所有表示方式都是等价的，彼此通过相似变换联系起来。

在 Weyl 表象中，根据 Pauli 矩阵的乘积关系 (3.48)，$\gamma^5$ 的具体形式为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\gamma^5 &= \mathrm{i}\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3 = \mathrm{i}\begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^1 \\ -\sigma^1 & \end{pmatrix}\gamma^2\gamma^3 = \mathrm{i}\begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^1 & \\ & \sigma^1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^2 \\ -\sigma^2 & \end{pmatrix}\gamma^3 \\
&= \mathrm{i}\begin{pmatrix} & -\mathrm{i}\sigma^3 \\ -\mathrm{i}\sigma^3 & \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^3 \\ -\sigma^3 & \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\mathbf{1} & \\ & \mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.73}
$$

用 $2 \times 2$ 单位矩阵 $\mathbf{1}$ 和 Pauli 矩阵定义

$$
\sigma^\mu \equiv (\mathbf{1}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}), \quad \bar{\sigma}^\mu \equiv (\mathbf{1}, -\boldsymbol{\sigma}),
\tag{4.74}
$$

那么，Dirac 矩阵表达式 (4.69) 可以简洁地归纳成

$$
\gamma^\mu = \begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^\mu \\ \bar{\sigma}^\mu & \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.75}
$$

生成元矩阵 $S^{\mu\nu}$ 表达为

$$
S^{\mu\nu} = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^\mu\bar{\sigma}^\nu - \sigma^\nu\bar{\sigma}^\mu & \\ & \bar{\sigma}^\mu\sigma^\nu - \bar{\sigma}^\nu\sigma^\mu \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.76}
$$

利用 (3.50) 式将 $S^{\mu\nu}$ 的空间分量化为

$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{ij} &= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}\begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^i\sigma^j + \sigma^j\sigma^i & \\ & -\sigma^i\sigma^j + \sigma^j\sigma^i \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}\begin{pmatrix} -2\mathrm{i}\varepsilon^{ijk}\sigma^k & \\ & -2\mathrm{i}\varepsilon^{ijk}\sigma^k \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon^{ijk}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^k & \\ & \sigma^k \end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.77}
$$

从 Pauli 矩阵的厄米性可知，$S^{ij}$ 是厄米矩阵，

$$
(S^{ij})^\dagger = S^{ij}.
\tag{4.78}
$$

由 (1.98) 式得

$$
S^i = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon^{ijk}S^{jk} = \frac{1}{4}\varepsilon^{ijk}\varepsilon^{jkl}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^l & \\ & \sigma^l \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{4}2\delta^{il}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^l & \\ & \sigma^l \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^i & \\ & \sigma^i \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.79}
$$

即 $S^i$ 是两个 SU(2) 群基础表示生成元 (3.45) 的直和，因此 $S^i$ 所属 SU(2) 群线性表示是两个 SU(2) 基础表示的直和。于是，自旋角动量矩阵 $\mathbf{S}$ 的平方为

$$
\mathbf{S}^2 = S^iS^i = \frac{1}{4}\begin{pmatrix} \sigma^i\sigma^i & \\ & \sigma^i\sigma^i \end{pmatrix} = \frac{3}{4}\begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1} & \\ & \mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2} + 1\right) = s(s + 1).
\tag{4.80}
$$

上式最后两步省略了 $4 \times 4$ 单位矩阵。可见，Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 的自旋量子数是

$$
s = \frac{1}{2}.
\tag{4.81}
$$

量子化之后，$\psi(x)$ 描述自旋为 $1/2$ 的粒子。

## 4.3 Dirac 方程

为了写下 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 的 Lorentz 不变拉氏量，我们需要结合两个旋量场来得到 Lorentz 标量。在 Weyl 表象中，$S^{\mu\nu}$ 的 $0i$ 分量为

$$
S^{0i} = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^0, \gamma^i] = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\gamma^0\gamma^i = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^i & \\ & \sigma^i \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.82}
$$

由 Pauli 矩阵的厄米性得

$$
(S^{0i})^\dagger = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -(\sigma^i)^\dagger & \\ & (\sigma^i)^\dagger \end{pmatrix} = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\sigma^i & \\ & \sigma^i \end{pmatrix} = -S^{0i}.
\tag{4.83}
$$

可见，$S^{0i}$ 不是厄米矩阵，而是反厄米矩阵。于是，当 $\omega_{0i} \neq 0$ 时，

$$
D^\dagger(\Lambda) = \left[\exp\left(-\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right)\right]^\dagger = \exp\left[\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})^\dagger\right] \neq \exp\left(\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right) = D^{-1}(\Lambda),
\tag{4.84}
$$

即 $D(\Lambda)$ 不是幺正矩阵。因此，$\psi^\dagger(x)\psi(x)$ 不是 Lorentz 标量：

$$
\psi'^{\dagger}(x')\psi'(x') = \psi^\dagger(x)D^\dagger(\Lambda)D(\Lambda)\psi(x) \neq \psi^\dagger(x)\psi(x).
\tag{4.85}
$$

根据 (4.5) 式，有

$$
(\gamma^0)^\dagger\gamma^0 = \gamma^0\gamma^0, \quad (\gamma^i)^\dagger\gamma^0 = -\gamma^i\gamma^0 = \gamma^0\gamma^i.
\tag{4.86}
$$

将这两条式子合起来写成一条常用的公式

$$
(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\gamma^0 = \gamma^0\gamma^\mu.
\tag{4.87}
$$

从而

$$
(S^{\mu\nu})^\dagger\gamma^0 = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu]^\dagger\gamma^0 = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}[(\gamma^\nu)^\dagger(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger - (\gamma^\mu)^\dagger(\gamma^\nu)^\dagger]\gamma^0 = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{4}\gamma^0(\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu - \gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu) = \gamma^0S^{\mu\nu},
\tag{4.88}
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
D^\dagger(\Lambda)\gamma^0 &= \exp\left[\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})^\dagger\right]\gamma^0 = \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}\left[\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})^\dagger\right]^n\gamma^0 = \gamma^0\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{n!}\left(\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right)^n \\
&= \gamma^0\exp\left(\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}\right) = \gamma^0D^{-1}(\Lambda).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.89}
$$

根据上式，定义 $\psi(x)$ 的 Dirac 共轭

$$
\bar{\psi}(x) \equiv \psi^\dagger(x)\gamma^0,
\tag{4.90}
$$

则相应的 Lorentz 变换形式为

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x') = \psi'^{\dagger}(x')\gamma^0 = \psi^\dagger(x)D^\dagger(\Lambda)\gamma^0 = \psi^\dagger(x)\gamma^0D^{-1}(\Lambda) = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda).
\tag{4.91}
$$

这样一来，$\bar{\psi}(x)\psi(x)$ 就是一个 Lorentz 标量：

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x')\psi'(x') = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = \bar{\psi}(x)\psi(x).
\tag{4.92}
$$

$\bar{\psi}(x)\psi(x)$ 这种形式的量称为旋量双线性型 (spinor bilinear)，利用 $\bar{\psi}(x)$ 还能构造 Lorentz 协变的其它旋量双线性型。$\bar{\psi}(x)\mathrm{i}\gamma^5\psi(x)$ 是一个 Lorentz 标量，

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x')\mathrm{i}\gamma^5\psi'(x') = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\mathrm{i}\gamma^5D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = \bar{\psi}(x)\mathrm{i}\gamma^5\psi(x).
\tag{4.93}
$$

$\bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\mu\psi(x)$ 和 $\bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi(x)$ 都是 Lorentz 矢量，

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x')\gamma^\mu\psi'(x') = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\nu\bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\nu\psi(x),
\tag{4.94}
$$

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x')\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi'(x') = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\nu\bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\nu\gamma^5\psi(x).
\tag{4.95}
$$

$\bar{\psi}(x)\sigma^{\mu\nu}\psi(x)$ 是一个 2 阶反对称 Lorentz 张量，

$$
\bar{\psi}'(x')\sigma^{\mu\nu}\psi'(x') = \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\sigma^{\mu\nu}D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = \Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\Lambda^\nu{}_\sigma\bar{\psi}(x)\sigma^{\rho\sigma}\psi(x).
\tag{4.96}
$$

如果将 $\psi(x)$ 看作旋量空间中的列矢量，则 $\psi^\dagger(x)$ 和 $\bar{\psi}(x)$ 都是行矢量，因而这些旋量双线性型都只是旋量空间中的 $1 \times 1$ 矩阵，也就是数。由 $\gamma^0$ 和 $\gamma^5$ 的厄米性及 (4.87) 式可知，这些旋量双线性型都是厄米的，也就是说，都是实数：

$$
(\bar{\psi}\psi)^\dagger = (\psi^\dagger\gamma^0\psi)^\dagger = \psi^\dagger\gamma^0\psi = \bar{\psi}\psi,
\tag{4.97}
$$

$$
(\bar{\psi}\mathrm{i}\gamma^5\psi)^\dagger = -\mathrm{i}\psi^\dagger\gamma^5\gamma^0\psi = \mathrm{i}\psi^\dagger\gamma^0\gamma^5\psi = \bar{\psi}\mathrm{i}\gamma^5\psi,
\tag{4.98}
$$

$$
(\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\psi)^\dagger = \psi^\dagger(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\gamma^0\psi = \psi^\dagger\gamma^0\gamma^\mu\psi = \bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\psi,
\tag{4.99}
$$

$$
(\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi)^\dagger = \psi^\dagger\gamma^5(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\gamma^0\psi = \psi^\dagger\gamma^5\gamma^0\gamma^\mu\psi = -\psi^\dagger\gamma^0\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\psi = \psi^\dagger\gamma^0\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi = \bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi,
\tag{4.100}
$$

$$
(\bar{\psi}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\psi)^\dagger = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\psi^\dagger[(\gamma^\nu)^\dagger(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger - (\gamma^\mu)^\dagger(\gamma^\nu)^\dagger]\gamma^0\psi = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\psi^\dagger\gamma^0(\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu - \gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)\psi = \bar{\psi}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\psi.
\tag{4.101}
$$

量子化之后，这五个旋量双线性型成为厄米算符。

此外，包含时空导数的旋量双线性型 $\bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi(x)$ 是 Lorentz 标量，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{\psi}'(x')\gamma^\mu\partial'_\mu\psi'(x') &= \bar{\psi}(x)D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda)(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu\partial_\nu\psi(x) = \bar{\psi}(x)\Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\gamma^\rho(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu\partial_\nu\psi(x) \\
&= \bar{\psi}(x)\delta^\nu{}_\rho\gamma^\rho\partial_\nu\psi(x) = \bar{\psi}(x)\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi(x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.102}
$$

利用 $\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi$ 和 $\bar{\psi}\psi$ 写下自由 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 的 Lorentz 不变拉氏量

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathrm{i}\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi - m\bar{\psi}\psi,
\tag{4.103}
$$

其中 $m > 0$ 是 Dirac 旋量场的质量。于是，

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_\mu\psi)} = \mathrm{i}\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu, \quad \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\psi} = -m\bar{\psi}.
\tag{4.104}
$$

Euler-Lagrange 方程 (1.142) 给出

$$
0 = \partial_\mu\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_\mu\psi)} - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\psi} = \mathrm{i}(\partial_\mu\bar{\psi})\gamma^\mu + m\bar{\psi}.
\tag{4.105}
$$

对上式取厄米共轭，得到

$$
0 = -\mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\partial_\mu(\psi^\dagger\gamma^0)^\dagger + m(\psi^\dagger\gamma^0)^\dagger = -\mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\gamma^0\partial_\mu\psi + m\gamma^0\psi = -\gamma^0(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi,
\tag{4.106}
$$

故 $\psi(x)$ 的经典运动方程为

$$
(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi(x) = 0.
\tag{4.107}
$$

上式就是 Dirac 方程 [3]，标明旋量指标的形式为

$$
[\mathrm{i}(\gamma^\mu)_{ab}\partial_\mu - m\delta_{ab}]\psi_b(x) = 0.
\tag{4.108}
$$

容易验证，Dirac 方程具有 Lorentz 协变性：

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial'_\mu - m)\psi'(x') &= [\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu\partial_\nu - m]D(\Lambda)\psi(x) = D(\Lambda)[\mathrm{i}D^{-1}(\Lambda)\gamma^\mu D(\Lambda)(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu\partial_\nu - m]\psi(x) \\
&= D(\Lambda)[\mathrm{i}\Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\gamma^\rho(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu\partial_\nu - m]\psi(x) = D(\Lambda)(\mathrm{i}\delta^\nu{}_\rho\gamma^\rho\partial_\nu - m)\psi(x) \\
&= D(\Lambda)(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\nu\partial_\nu - m)\psi(x) = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.109}
$$

对 Dirac 方程 (4.107) 左边乘以 $(-\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)$，利用反对易关系 (4.1)，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &= (-\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\nu\partial_\nu - m)\psi = (\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\partial_\mu\partial_\nu + m^2)\psi = \left[\frac{1}{2}\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu(\partial_\mu\partial_\nu + \partial_\nu\partial_\mu) + m^2\right]\psi \\
&= \left[\frac{1}{2}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu + \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu)\partial_\mu\partial_\nu + m^2\right]\psi = (g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu + m^2)\psi = (\partial^2 + m^2)\psi.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.110}
$$

也就是说，自由的 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 满足 Klein-Gordon 方程

$$
(\partial^2 + m^2)\psi(x) = 0.
\tag{4.111}
$$

(4.76) 式表明，旋量表示的生成元在 Weyl 表象中是分块对角的，因而可将旋量表示分解为两个 2 维表示的直和。相应地，可以把具有 4 个分量的 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi$ 分解为两个二分量旋量场 $\eta_{\mathrm{L}}$ 和 $\eta_{\mathrm{R}}$：

$$
\psi = \begin{pmatrix} \eta_{\mathrm{L}} \\ \eta_{\mathrm{R}} \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.112}
$$

这样的二分量旋量称为 Weyl 旋量，其中，$\eta_{\mathrm{L}}$ 称为左手 (left-handed) Weyl 旋量，$\eta_{\mathrm{R}}$ 称为右手 (right-handed) Weyl 旋量。

利用 (4.75) 式将 Dirac 方程 (4.107) 化为

$$
0 = (\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi = \begin{pmatrix} -m & \mathrm{i}\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu \\ \mathrm{i}\bar{\sigma}^\mu\partial_\mu & -m \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \eta_{\mathrm{L}} \\ \eta_{\mathrm{R}} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{i}\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{R}} - m\eta_{\mathrm{L}} \\ \mathrm{i}\bar{\sigma}^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{L}} - m\eta_{\mathrm{R}} \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.113}
$$

即

$$
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{i}\bar{\sigma}^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{L}} - m\eta_{\mathrm{R}} = 0, \\
\mathrm{i}\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{R}} - m\eta_{\mathrm{L}} = 0.
\end{cases}
\tag{4.114}
$$

这是两个相互耦合的方程。如果 $m = 0$，两个方程就各自独立了：

$$
\mathrm{i}\bar{\sigma}^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{L}} = 0, \quad \mathrm{i}\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu\eta_{\mathrm{R}} = 0.
\tag{4.115}
$$

这两个独立方程称为 Weyl 方程 [18]。可见，非零质量 $m$ 的存在将左手和右手 Weyl 旋量场耦合起来。$S^i$ 的直和分解 (4.79) 表明，左手和右手 Weyl 旋量各自对应于一个 SU(2) 群基础表示。当 $m = 0$ 时，量子化之后的 $\eta_{\mathrm{L}}(x)$ 和 $\eta_{\mathrm{R}}(x)$ 各自描述自旋为 $1/2$ 的粒子。

## 4.4 Dirac 旋量场的平面波展开

### 4.4.1 平面波解的一般形式

本小节讨论与表象选取无关。

自由 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi_a(x)$ 满足 Klein-Gordon 方程，因而具有平面波解。对于确定的动量 $\mathbf{k}$，我们假设 Dirac 方程具有如下形式的平面波解：

$$
\varphi_a(x, \mathbf{k}) = w_a(k^0, \mathbf{k})\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}k\cdot x}.
\tag{4.116}
$$

其中，系数 $w_a(k^0, \mathbf{k})$ 是四分量的旋量，带着一个旋量指标 $a$。隐去旋量指标，将这个平面波解代入到 Dirac 方程 (4.107) 中，得到

$$
0 = (\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\varphi(x, \mathbf{k}) = (\gamma^\mu k_\mu - m)w(k^0, \mathbf{k})\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}k\cdot x} = (k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)w(k^0, \mathbf{k})\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}k\cdot x}.
\tag{4.117}
$$

因此

$$
(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)w(k^0, \mathbf{k}) = 0.
\tag{4.118}
$$

对上式左乘 $\gamma^0$，得

$$
[k^0 - \gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m\gamma^0]w(k^0, \mathbf{k}) = 0.
\tag{4.119}
$$

移项，将上式化为

$$
[\gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) + m\gamma^0]w(k^0, \mathbf{k}) = k^0w(k^0, \mathbf{k}).
\tag{4.120}
$$

这是矩阵 $[\gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) + m\gamma^0]$ 的本征值方程，具有非平庸解的条件是特征多项式 $\det[k^0 - \gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m\gamma^0]$ 为零，即

$$
\det[k^0 - \gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m\gamma^0] = 0.
\tag{4.121}
$$

这个方程的根给出 $k^0$ 的本征值，相应的非平庸解是本征矢量。

利用 $(\gamma^0)^2 = \mathbf{1}$，将方程 (4.121) 化为

$$
0 = \det[k^0\mathbf{1} - \gamma^0(\mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m\gamma^0] = \det[\gamma^0(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)] = \det(\gamma^0)\det(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m).
\tag{4.122}
$$

由 $[\det(\gamma^0)]^2 = \det(\gamma^0\gamma^0) = \det(\mathbf{1}) = 1$ 知道 $\det(\gamma^0) \neq 0$。因而方程 (4.121) 等价于

$$
\det(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m) = 0.
\tag{4.123}
$$

利用 $(\gamma^5)^2 = \mathbf{1}$，将上式左边化为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m) &= \det[(\gamma^5)^2(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)] = \det[\gamma^5(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)\gamma^5] \\
&= \det[(\gamma^5)^2(-k^0\gamma^0 + \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)] = \det[-(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.124}
$$

这里第二步用到行列式性质

$$
\det(AB) = \det(BA),
\tag{4.125}
$$

第三步用到 $\gamma^5$ 与 $\gamma^\mu$ 反对易的性质 (4.41)。由反对易关系 (4.1) 有

$$
(k_\mu\gamma^\mu)^2 = k_\mu k_\nu\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu = \frac{1}{2}k_\mu k_\nu(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu + \gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu) = k_\mu k_\nu g^{\mu\nu}\mathbf{1} = k^2\mathbf{1} = [(k^0)^2 - |\mathbf{k}|^2]\mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.126}
$$

从而，

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\det(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)]^2 &= \det[(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m)]\det[-(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma}) - m] \\
&= \det(k_\mu\gamma^\mu - m)\det(-k_\nu\gamma^\nu - m) = \det[(k_\mu\gamma^\mu - m)(-k_\nu\gamma^\nu - m)] \\
&= \det[-(k_\mu\gamma^\mu)^2 + m^2] = \det\{[-(k^0)^2 + |\mathbf{k}|^2 + m^2]\mathbf{1}\} \\
&= [-(k^0)^2 + |\mathbf{k}|^2 + m^2]^4 = [E_{\mathbf{k}}^2 - (k^0)^2]^4,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.127}
$$

其中 $E_{\mathbf{k}} \equiv \sqrt{|\mathbf{k}|^2 + m^2}$。于是，方程 (4.123) 化为

$$
0 = \det(k^0\gamma^0 - \mathbf{k}\cdot\boldsymbol{\gamma} - m) = [E_{\mathbf{k}}^2 - (k^0)^2]^2 = (E_{\mathbf{k}} + k^0)^2(E_{\mathbf{k}} - k^0)^2.
\tag{4.128}
$$

这个方程有 2 个根 $k^0 = \pm E_{\mathbf{k}}$；这 2 个根都是 2 重根，各自对应于 2 个线性独立的本征矢量；一共有 4 个线性独立的本征矢量。

(1) $k^0 = E_{\mathbf{k}}$ 对应于 2 个本征矢量

$$
w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma), \quad \sigma = 1, 2.
\tag{4.129}
$$

因而平面波解中有 2 个正能解，形式为

$$
w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t - \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})], \quad \sigma = 1, 2.
\tag{4.130}
$$

(2) $k^0 = -E_{\mathbf{k}}$ 对应于 2 个本征矢量

$$
w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma), \quad \sigma = 1, 2.
\tag{4.131}
$$

因而平面波解中有 2 个负能解，形式为

$$
w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t + \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})], \quad \sigma = 1, 2.
\tag{4.132}
$$

将这 4 个本征矢量的正交归一关系取为

$$
\begin{aligned}
w^{(+)\dagger}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma') &= 2E_{\mathbf{k}}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}, \quad w^{(-)\dagger}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma') = 2E_{\mathbf{k}}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}, \\
w^{(+)\dagger}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma') &= w^{(-)\dagger}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma') = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.133}
$$

引入四分量旋量 $u(\mathbf{k}, \sigma)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{k}, \sigma)$，定义为

$$
u(\mathbf{k}, \sigma) \equiv w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma), \quad v(\mathbf{k}, \sigma) \equiv w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, -\mathbf{k}, \sigma), \quad \sigma = 1, 2.
\tag{4.134}
$$

于是，Dirac 方程的正能解和负能解可以分别写作

$$
\varphi^{(+)}(x, \mathbf{k}, \sigma) \equiv w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t - \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})] = u(\mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t - \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})],
\tag{4.135}
$$

$$
\varphi^{(-)}(x, \mathbf{k}, \sigma) \equiv w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{k}}, -\mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t - \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})] = v(\mathbf{k}, \sigma)\exp[\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{k}}t - \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x})].
\tag{4.136}
$$

替换动量记号，得到

$$
\varphi^{(+)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \sigma) = u(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}, \quad \varphi^{(-)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \sigma) = v(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}, \quad p^0 = E_{\mathbf{p}} \equiv \sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2 + m^2} > 0.
\tag{4.137}
$$

从而，Dirac 旋量场算符 $\psi(\mathbf{x}, t)$ 的平面波展开式可写作

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi(\mathbf{x}, t) &= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\sigma=1}^2\left[\varphi^{(+)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \sigma)c_{\mathbf{p},\sigma} + \varphi^{(-)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \sigma)d^\dagger_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}\right] \\
&= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\sigma=1}^2\left[u(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)c_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)d^\dagger_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.138}
$$

其中，$c_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}$ 是湮灭算符，$d^\dagger_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}$ 是产生算符，而且 $c_{\mathbf{p},\sigma} \neq d_{\mathbf{p},\sigma}$。平面波旋量系数 $u(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)$ 的正交归一关系为

$$
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)u(\mathbf{p}, \sigma') = w^{(+)\dagger}(E_{\mathbf{p}}, \mathbf{p}, \sigma)w^{(+)}(E_{\mathbf{p}}, \mathbf{p}, \sigma') = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'},
\tag{4.139}
$$

$$
v^\dagger(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)v(\mathbf{p}, \sigma') = w^{(-)\dagger}(-E_{\mathbf{p}}, -\mathbf{p}, \sigma)w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{p}}, -\mathbf{p}, \sigma') = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'},
\tag{4.140}
$$

$$
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p}, \sigma)v(-\mathbf{p}, \sigma') = w^{(+)\dagger}(E_{\mathbf{p}}, \mathbf{p}, \sigma)w^{(-)}(-E_{\mathbf{p}}, \mathbf{p}, \sigma') = 0.
\tag{4.141}
$$

### 4.4.2 Weyl 表象中的平面波解

本小节在 Weyl 表象中讨论 Dirac 方程的平面波解。

Dirac 旋量场描述自旋为 $1/2$ 的有质量粒子，根据 3.1.3 小节讨论，这样的粒子具有 2 种自旋极化态，对应于螺旋度的 2 种本征值 $+1/2$ 和 $-1/2$。为便于表述，这里我们采用归一化的螺旋度本征值 $\lambda = \pm$。类似于矢量场情况，$\lambda = -$ 是左旋极化，$\lambda = +$ 是右旋极化。因此，无论是平面波解的正能解还是负能解，都能够以 2 种螺旋度本征态作为 2 个线性独立的本征矢量。

按照这个思路，把 2 个正能解表达为

$$
\varphi^{(+)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \lambda) = u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}, \quad \lambda = \pm, \quad p^0 = E_{\mathbf{p}} = \sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2 + m^2}.
\tag{4.142}
$$

根据 Dirac 方程 (4.107)，有

$$
0 = (\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\varphi^{(+)}(x, \mathbf{p}, \lambda) = (p_\mu\gamma^\mu - m)u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x},
\tag{4.143}
$$

即 $u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda)$ 满足运动方程

$$
(\not{p} - m)u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda) = 0,
\tag{4.144}
$$

其中 $\not{p}$ 的定义为

$$
\not{p} \equiv p_\mu\gamma^\mu.
\tag{4.145}
$$

这种斜线记号称为 Dirac 斜线 (slash)，是 Richard Feynman 引进的。

将四分量旋量 $u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda)$ 分解为两个二分量旋量 $f_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 和 $g_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，

$$
u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.146}
$$

那么，根据 Weyl 表象中的 Dirac 矩阵表达式 (4.75)，方程 (4.144) 化为

$$
0 = (\not{p} - m)u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} -m & \sigma^\mu p_\mu \\ \bar{\sigma}^\mu p_\mu & -m \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} p_\mu\sigma^\mu g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mf_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ p_\mu\bar{\sigma}^\mu f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mg_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.147}
$$

即

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mf_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0,
\tag{4.148}
$$

$$
(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mg_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0.
\tag{4.149}
$$

将 (4.75) 式代入反对易关系 (4.1)，得

$$
2g^{\mu\nu}\begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1} & \\ & \mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix} = \{\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu\} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma^\mu\bar{\sigma}^\nu + \sigma^\nu\bar{\sigma}^\mu & \\ & \bar{\sigma}^\mu\sigma^\nu + \bar{\sigma}^\nu\sigma^\mu \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.150}
$$

故

$$
\sigma^\mu\bar{\sigma}^\nu + \sigma^\nu\bar{\sigma}^\mu = 2g^{\mu\nu}, \quad \bar{\sigma}^\mu\sigma^\nu + \bar{\sigma}^\nu\sigma^\mu = 2g^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{4.151}
$$

因此，

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)(p\cdot\bar{\sigma}) = p_\mu p_\nu\sigma^\mu\bar{\sigma}^\nu = \frac{1}{2}p_\mu p_\nu(\sigma^\mu\bar{\sigma}^\nu + \sigma^\nu\bar{\sigma}^\mu) = \frac{1}{2}p_\mu p_\nu 2g^{\mu\nu} = p^2 = m^2.
\tag{4.152}
$$

由方程 (4.149) 得

$$
g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \frac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.153}
$$

将上式代入到方程 (4.148) 左边，得

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)g_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mf_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \frac{(p\cdot\sigma)(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})}{m}f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mf_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \frac{m^2}{m}f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - mf_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0.
\tag{4.154}
$$

可见，关系式 (4.153) 也符合方程 (4.148)。于是，任取非零 $f_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 都能使

$$
u(\mathbf{p}, \lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \dfrac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.155}
$$

满足运动方程 (4.144)。

在旋量表示中，螺旋度矩阵是自旋角动量矩阵 $\mathbf{S}$ 在动量 $\mathbf{p}$ 方向上的投影，即 $\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S}$。对于 Weyl 表象，根据 (4.79) 式，有

$$
\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} & \\ & \hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.156}
$$

因而归一化螺旋度矩阵为

$$
2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S} = \begin{pmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} & \\ & \hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.157}
$$

上式的两个分块相同，因而左手和右手 Weyl 旋量对应的归一化螺旋度矩阵都是 $\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}$，由 Pauli 矩阵的定义 (3.46) 得

$$
\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \frac{\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{|\mathbf{p}|} = \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|}\begin{pmatrix} p^3 & p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 & -p^3 \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.158}
$$

引入归一化螺旋度矩阵 $\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}$ 的本征矢量 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，称为螺旋态，满足本征值方程

$$
(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \lambda\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}), \quad \lambda = \pm.
\tag{4.159}
$$

求解这个方程，得到归一化本征矢量

$$
\xi_+(\mathbf{p}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \xi_-(\mathbf{p}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} -p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.160}
$$

这是螺旋态 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 的具体形式。它们满足正交归一关系

$$
\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) = \delta_{\lambda\lambda'},
\tag{4.161}
$$

和完备性关系

$$
\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) = \mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.162}
$$

此外，由 $\hat{\mathbf{p}} = \mathbf{p}/|\mathbf{p}|$ 得

$$
(\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \lambda|\mathbf{p}|\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.163}
$$

容易验证，(4.160) 式表达的螺旋态 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 确实是 $\lambda = \pm$ 的本征态，

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_+(\mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^3 & p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 & -p^3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^3(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3) + (p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2)(p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2) \\ (p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2)(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3) - p^3(p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^3|\mathbf{p}| + |\mathbf{p}|^2 \\ (p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2)|\mathbf{p}| \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^3 + |\mathbf{p}| \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= +\xi_+(\mathbf{p}),
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.164}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_-(\mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^3 & p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 & -p^3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} -p^3(p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2) + (p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2)(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3) \\ (p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2)(-p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2) - p^3(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{1}{|\mathbf{p}|\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} (p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2)|\mathbf{p}| \\ -|\mathbf{p}|^2 - p^3|\mathbf{p}| \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}}\begin{pmatrix} p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ -|\mathbf{p}| - p^3 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= -\xi_-(\mathbf{p}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.165}
$$

而且符合正交归一关系 (4.161)，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\xi_+^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_+(\mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}\begin{pmatrix} |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 & p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \\ p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)^2 + |p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2|^2}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)} = \frac{2|\mathbf{p}|^2 + 2p^3|\mathbf{p}|}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)} = 1,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.166}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\xi_-^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_-(\mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}\begin{pmatrix} -p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 & |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{|-p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2|^2 + (|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)^2}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)} = \frac{2|\mathbf{p}|^2 + 2p^3|\mathbf{p}|}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)} = 1,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.167}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\xi_+^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_-(\mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)}\begin{pmatrix} |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 & p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -p^1 + \mathrm{i}p^2 \\ |\mathbf{p}| + p^3 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{-(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)(p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2) + (|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)(p^1 - \mathrm{i}p^2)}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}| + p^3)} = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.168}
$$

也满足完备性关系 (4.162)，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) &= \xi_+(\mathbf{p})\xi_+^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) + \xi_-(\mathbf{p})\xi_-^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \\
&= \frac{1}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)}\begin{pmatrix} (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)^2 + |-p^1+\mathrm{i}p^2|^2 & (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)(p^1-\mathrm{i}p^2) + (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)(-p^1+\mathrm{i}p^2) \\ (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)(p^1+\mathrm{i}p^2) + (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)(-p^1-\mathrm{i}p^2) & |p^1+\mathrm{i}p^2|^2 + (|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)^2 \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \frac{1}{2|\mathbf{p}|(|\mathbf{p}|+p^3)}\begin{pmatrix} 2|\mathbf{p}|^2+2p^3|\mathbf{p}| & \\ & 2|\mathbf{p}|^2+2p^3|\mathbf{p}| \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \\ & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \mathbf{1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.169}
$$

为了让 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 作为螺旋度本征态，我们设 $f_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 正比于 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，

$$
f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = C_\lambda\,\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.170}
$$

利用 (4.163) 式，将 (4.155) 式化为

$$
u(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \frac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}f_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = C_\lambda\begin{pmatrix} \xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{m}\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = C_\lambda\begin{pmatrix} \xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m}\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.171}
$$

为了让 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 满足正交归一关系 (4.139)，我们取

$$
C_\lambda = \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|},
\tag{4.172}
$$

则由

$$
\sqrt{(E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)(E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)} = \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}^2-\lambda^2|\mathbf{p}|^2} = \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}^2-|\mathbf{p}|^2} = m
\tag{4.173}
$$

有

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_\lambda\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m} &= \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m} = \frac{\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}}{m}\sqrt{(E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)(E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)} \\
&= \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.174}
$$

于是得到 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 的螺旋态表达式

$$
u(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.175}
$$

其中，$\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 定义为

$$
\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \equiv \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|},
\tag{4.176}
$$

它是关于 $\mathbf{p}$ 的偶函数，

$$
\omega_\lambda(-\mathbf{p}) = \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.177}
$$

这样的话，根据 (4.157) 式，$u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 是螺旋度本征态，本征值为 $\lambda$：

$$
(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S})u(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \lambda\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \lambda\,u(\mathbf{p},\lambda).
\tag{4.178}
$$

另一方面，将 2 个负能解表达为

$$
\varphi^{(-)}(x,\mathbf{p},\lambda) = v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x},\quad \lambda=\pm,\quad p^0 = E_{\mathbf{p}} = \sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2+m^2}.
\tag{4.179}
$$

根据 Dirac 方程 (4.107)，有

$$
0 = (\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\varphi^{(-)}(x,\mathbf{p},\lambda) = (-p_\mu\gamma^\mu - m)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x},
\tag{4.180}
$$

即 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 满足运动方程

$$
(\not{p}+m)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = 0.
\tag{4.181}
$$

同样，将四分量旋量 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 分解为两个二分量旋量 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 和 $\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，

$$
v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.182}
$$

则有

$$
0 = (\not{p}+m)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} m & \sigma^\mu p_\mu \\ \bar{\sigma}^\mu p_\mu & m \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} p_\mu\sigma^\mu\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ p_\mu\bar{\sigma}^\mu\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.183}
$$

即

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0,
\tag{4.184}
$$

$$
(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0.
\tag{4.185}
$$

由方程 (4.185) 得

$$
\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = -\frac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.186}
$$

将上式代入到方程 (4.184) 左边，由 (4.152) 式得到

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)\tilde{g}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = -\frac{(p\cdot\sigma)(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})}{m}\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = -\frac{m^2}{m}\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + m\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = 0.
\tag{4.187}
$$

可见，关系式 (4.186) 符合方程 (4.184)。于是，任取非零 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 都能使

$$
v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\frac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.188}
$$

满足运动方程 (4.181)。

为了让 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 作为螺旋度本征态，我们设 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 正比于 $\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})$，

$$
\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \tilde{C}_\lambda\,\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.189}
$$

在这里我们没有选择让 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 正比于 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，原因将在 4.5.4 小节中说明，现在姑且接受这种选择。从而，

$$
v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\frac{p\cdot\bar{\sigma}}{m}\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \tilde{C}_\lambda\begin{pmatrix} \xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{m}\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \tilde{C}_\lambda\begin{pmatrix} \xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m}\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.190}
$$

为了让 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 满足正交归一关系 (4.140)，我们取

$$
\tilde{C}_\lambda = \lambda\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|},
\tag{4.191}
$$

则由

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\tilde{C}_\lambda\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m} &= -\lambda\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\frac{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}{m} = -\lambda\frac{\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}}{m}\sqrt{(E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)(E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)} \\
&= -\lambda\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.192}
$$

得到 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 的螺旋态表达式

$$
v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda\sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|}\,\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.193}
$$

这样一来，$v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 是螺旋度本征态，本征值为 $-\lambda$：

$$
(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S})v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = -\lambda\begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} = -\lambda\,v(\mathbf{p},\lambda).
\tag{4.194}
$$

根据 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 的正交归一关系 (4.161)，可以验证，$u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 满足 (4.139) 和 (4.140) 形式的正交归一关系：

$$
\begin{aligned}
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= [\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) = [\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} \\
&= [\omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = [(E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|) + (E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.195}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \lambda'\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda'\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \lambda\lambda'[\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) = \lambda\lambda'[\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} \\
&= \lambda^2[\omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = [(E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|) + (E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.196}
$$

依照螺旋态的本征值方程 (4.159)，得到

$$
(-\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p}) = -\lambda\,\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p}),
\tag{4.197}
$$

从而

$$
(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p}) = \lambda\,\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p}).
\tag{4.198}
$$

可见，$\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p})$ 与 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 服从相同的本征值方程，这意味着 $\xi_{-\lambda}(-\mathbf{p}) \propto \xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，故

$$
\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p}) \propto \xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) = \delta_{\lambda\lambda'}.
\tag{4.199}
$$

于是，(4.141) 形式的正交关系也成立：

$$
\begin{aligned}
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \lambda'\omega_{\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda'\omega_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \lambda'[\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p}) - \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p})]\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(-\mathbf{p}) \\
&\propto \lambda'[\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) - \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} \\
&= \lambda[\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) - \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.200}
$$

整理一下，平面波旋量系数 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 满足正交归一关系

$$
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},\quad u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda') = v^\dagger(-\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = 0.
\tag{4.201}
$$

此外，由 (4.173) 式有

$$
\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = \sqrt{(E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)(E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)} = m.
\tag{4.202}
$$

利用

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) &= u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\gamma^0 = \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.203}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) &= v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\gamma^0 = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.204}
$$

推出

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= [\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = 2\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = 2m\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.205}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \lambda'\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda'\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= -\lambda\lambda'[\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} = -2\lambda^2\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\delta_{\lambda\lambda'} \\
&= -2m\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.206}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \lambda'\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda'\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \lambda'[\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) - \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\
&= \lambda'[\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) - \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda,-\lambda'} \\
&= -\lambda[\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) - \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{\lambda,-\lambda'} = 0,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.207}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') &= \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \lambda[-\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) \\
&= \lambda[-\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda'}(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{\lambda'}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{-\lambda,\lambda'} \\
&= \lambda[-\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) + \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})]\delta_{-\lambda,\lambda'} = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.208}
$$

整理一下，有

$$
\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = 2m\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},\quad \bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = -2m\delta_{\lambda\lambda'},\quad \bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = \bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda') = 0.
\tag{4.209}
$$

这些等式是 Lorentz 不变的。

另一方面，利用等式

$$
(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}+\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}),
\tag{4.210}
$$

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}-\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) = \omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}),
\tag{4.211}
$$

以及 (4.202) 式和 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 的完备性关系 (4.162)，有

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\lambda=\pm}u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) &= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} \omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \\ \omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} m\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & (p\cdot\sigma)\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \\ (p\cdot\bar{\sigma})\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & m\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p})\xi_\lambda^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \begin{pmatrix} m & p\cdot\sigma \\ p\cdot\bar{\sigma} & m \end{pmatrix} = p_\mu\gamma^\mu + m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.212}
$$

通过等式

$$
(p\cdot\bar{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}+\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}-\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = \omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}),
\tag{4.213}
$$

$$
(p\cdot\sigma)\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}-\mathbf{p}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = (E_{\mathbf{p}}+\lambda|\mathbf{p}|)\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) = \omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}),
\tag{4.214}
$$

得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\lambda=\pm}v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) &= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -\lambda\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \lambda\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} -\lambda^2\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & \lambda^2\omega_\lambda^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \\ -\lambda^2\omega_{-\lambda}^2(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & -\lambda^2\omega_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\omega_\lambda(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\begin{pmatrix} -m\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & (p\cdot\sigma)\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \\ (p\cdot\bar{\sigma})\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) & -m\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})\xi_{-\lambda}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}) \end{pmatrix} \\
&= \begin{pmatrix} -m & p\cdot\sigma \\ p\cdot\bar{\sigma} & -m \end{pmatrix} = p_\mu\gamma^\mu - m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.215}
$$

整理一下，有如下螺旋度求和关系，或者说，自旋求和关系：

$$
\sum_{\lambda=\pm}u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \not{p}+m,\quad \sum_{\lambda=\pm}v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda) = \not{p}-m.
\tag{4.216}
$$

用 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 把 Dirac 旋量场算符 $\psi(\mathbf{x},t)$ 的平面波展开式写作

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi(\mathbf{x},t) &= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[\varphi^{(+)}(x,\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} + \varphi^{(-)}(x,\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right] \\
&= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right],
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.217}
$$

其中 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 是湮灭算符，$b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 是产生算符，而且 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} \ne b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$。于是

$$
\psi^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t) = \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right],
\tag{4.218}
$$

$$
\bar{\psi}(\mathbf{x},t) = \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + \bar{v}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right].
\tag{4.219}
$$

### 4.4.3 哈密顿量和产生湮灭算符

根据 (1.143) 和 (4.104) 式，$\psi(x)$ 对应的共轭动量密度是

$$
\pi = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_0\psi)} = \mathrm{i}\bar{\psi}\gamma^0 = \mathrm{i}\psi^\dagger,
\tag{4.220}
$$

它的平面波展开式为

$$
\pi(\mathbf{x},t) = \mathrm{i}\psi^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t) = \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{\mathrm{i}}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right].
\tag{4.221}
$$

自由运动的旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 满足 Dirac 方程 (4.107)，相应地，拉氏量 (4.103) 化为

$$
\mathcal{L} = \bar{\psi}(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0.
\tag{4.222}
$$

根据 (1.145) 式，自由 Dirac 旋量场的哈密顿量密度为

$$
\mathcal{H} = \pi\partial_0\psi - \mathcal{L} = \pi\partial_0\psi = \mathrm{i}\psi^\dagger\partial_0\psi.
\tag{4.223}
$$

从而，哈密顿量为

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathcal{H} = \int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\psi^\dagger\mathrm{i}\partial_0\psi \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right] \\
&\qquad\qquad\times q_0\left[u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}q\cdot x} - v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}q\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q\,q_0}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x}\right. \\
&\qquad - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x} - u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x} \\
&\qquad\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q\,E_{\mathbf{q}}}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left\{\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right.\right. \\
&\qquad\left.\left. - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right]\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + \delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q})\left[-u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right.\right. \\
&\qquad\left.\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right]\right\} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{2}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right. \\
&\qquad\left. - u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{2}\left(2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} - 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{p}}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.224}
$$

倒数第二步用到正交归一关系 (4.201)。

另一方面，利用正交归一关系 (4.201)，得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\psi(\mathbf{x},t) &= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x} + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q})\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t}\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left(2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}\right) = \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.225}
$$

从而将湮灭算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 和产生算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 表示为

$$
a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\psi(\mathbf{x},t),\quad a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\psi^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda).
\tag{4.226}
$$

同理推出

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\psi(\mathbf{x},t) &= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q})\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left[v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda'=\pm}\left(2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right) = \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.227}
$$

于是将产生算符 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 和湮灭算符 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 表示成

$$
b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\psi(\mathbf{x},t),\quad b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\psi^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda).
\tag{4.228}
$$

## 4.5 Dirac 旋量场的正则量子化

### 4.5.1 用等时对易关系量子化 Dirac 旋量场的困难

在标量场和矢量场的正则量子化程序中，我们先假设场算符与其共轭动量密度算符满足等时对易关系 (2.74)，然后推导出产生湮灭算符的对易关系，再通过计算给出正定的哈密顿量（对于无质量矢量场，需要用弱 Lorentz 规范条件来得到正的哈密顿量期待值），从而说明在量子场论中使用正则量子化方法是合理的。在本小节中，我们将尝试用类似的等时对易关系对 Dirac 旋量场进行量子化，不过，我们会发现这种方法并不能给出正定的哈密顿量，因而是不可行的。

假设 Dirac 旋量场算符 $\psi(x)$ 与其共轭动量密度算符 $\pi(x)$ 满足等时对易关系

$$
[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\pi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = \mathrm{i}\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}),\quad [\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = [\pi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\pi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = 0.
\tag{4.229}
$$

这里已经将旋量指标明显地写出来。根据 (4.220) 式，这些关系等价于 $\psi(x)$ 与 $\psi^\dagger(x)$ 的等时对易关系

$$
[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)] = \delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}),\quad [\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = [\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)] = 0.
\tag{4.230}
$$

接下来，我们计算产生湮灭算符的对易关系。由 (4.226) 式和正交归一关系 (4.201)，可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)]u_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\cdot\mathbf{x}}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{1}{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda')(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) = (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.231}
$$

另外，有

$$
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = 0.
\tag{4.232}
$$

由 (4.228) 式和正交归一关系 (4.201)，可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')[\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)]v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda)(-\delta_{ba})\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) \\
&= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\cdot\mathbf{x}}v^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) \\
&= -\frac{1}{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda')v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) = -(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.233}
$$

这个结果非同寻常地多了一个负号。此外，还有

$$
[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}[\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)]v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') = 0,
\tag{4.234}
$$

$$
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)] = 0,
\tag{4.235}
$$

以及

$$
\begin{aligned}
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)]v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q})\cdot\mathbf{x}}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{\mathrm{e}^{2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t}}{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q}) = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.236}
$$

上式最后一步用到正交归一关系 (4.201)。

整理起来，通过等时对易关系 (4.229) 得到的产生湮灭算符对易关系为

$$
\begin{aligned}
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] &= (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),\quad [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] = [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] = 0, \\
[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] &= -(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),\quad [b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] = [b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] = 0, \\
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] &= [b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] = [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}] = [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger] = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.237}
$$

利用这样的对易关系，可以把哈密顿量 (4.224) 化为

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{p}}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{p}}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right) + (2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{0})\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}2E_{\mathbf{p}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.238}
$$

上式最后一行第二项是零点能。在第一项中由 $(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 描述的粒子对总能量的贡献为正，但由 $(b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 描述的粒子对总能量的贡献为负。从而，粒子数密度 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 越大，场的总能量越少，这显然是非物理的。因此，用正则对易关系 (4.229) 对 Dirac 旋量场进行量子化是行不通的。

### 4.5.2 用等时反对易关系量子化 Dirac 旋量场

从 (4.238) 式的计算过程可以看出，如果在交换 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 和 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 位置的同时可以改变圆括号中第二项的符号，就可以得到正定的哈密顿量。这意味着我们需要的不是 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 与 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 的对易关系，而是反对易关系。为了得到合适的 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 与 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 的反对易关系，则需要舍弃等时对易关系 (4.229)，代之以等时反对易关系

$$
\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\pi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = \mathrm{i}\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}),\quad \{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = \{\pi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\pi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = 0.
\tag{4.239}
$$

采用反对易关系进行量子化的方法称为 Jordan-Wigner 量子化。根据 (4.220) 式，这些关系等价于 $\psi(x)$ 与 $\psi^\dagger(x)$ 的等时反对易关系

$$
\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)\} = \delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}),\quad \{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = \{\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)\} = 0.
\tag{4.240}
$$

接下来，我们计算产生湮灭算符的反对易关系。计算过程与上一小节类似，只是我们要将 (4.231) 至 (4.236) 式中表示对易的方括号改成表示反对易的花括号。由此得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)\}u_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) \\
&= (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.241}
$$

和

$$
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = 0.
\tag{4.242}
$$

另外，有

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\{\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\}v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\delta_{ba}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\cdot\mathbf{x}}v^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')v(\mathbf{p},\lambda) \\
&= \frac{1}{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda')v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) = (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.243}
$$

与 (4.233) 式不同，上式结果具有正常的符号。此外，还有

$$
\{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}\{\psi_a^\dagger(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)\}v_a(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') = 0,
\tag{4.244}
$$

$$
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x-q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b^\dagger(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b(\mathbf{y},t)\} = 0,
\tag{4.245}
$$

以及

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\{\psi_a(\mathbf{x},t),\psi_b^\dagger(\mathbf{y},t)\}v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda') \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3y\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p\cdot x+q\cdot y)}u_a^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v_b(\mathbf{q},\lambda')\delta_{ab}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}) = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.246}
$$

整理起来，通过等时反对易关系 (4.239) 得到的产生湮灭算符反对易关系为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} &= (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),\quad \{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} = \{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} = 0, \\
\{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} &= (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),\quad \{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} = \{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} = 0, \\
\{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} &= \{b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} = \{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\} = \{a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\} = 0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.247}
$$

$(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 和 $(b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 各自描述一种粒子。利用这样的反对易关系，把哈密顿量 (4.224) 化为

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{p}}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{p}}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} + b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right) - (2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{0})\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}2E_{\mathbf{p}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.248}
$$

上式最后一行第二项是零点能。第一项是所有动量模式所有螺旋度所有粒子贡献的能量之和，它是正定的。可见，用等时反对易关系对 Dirac 旋量场进行正则量子化是合适的。

利用 (4.9) 式和反对易关系 (4.247)，可得哈密顿量 $H$ 与产生湮灭算符的对易子为

$$
\begin{aligned}
[H,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger] &= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left[a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'} + b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}, a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left(a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\{a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\} - \{a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\right. \\
&\qquad\qquad\left. + b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\{b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\} - \{b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\{a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\} = \sum_{\lambda'}\int\mathrm{d}^3q\,E_{\mathbf{q}}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{p}) = E_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.249}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[H,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}] &= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left[a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'} + b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}, a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right] = \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left(-\{a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\right) \\
&= -\sum_{\lambda'}\int\mathrm{d}^3q\,E_{\mathbf{q}}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{p}) = -E_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.250}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[H,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger] &= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left[a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'} + b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}, b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right] = \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\{b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\mathrm{d}^3q\,E_{\mathbf{q}}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{p}) = E_{\mathbf{p}}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.251}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[H,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}] &= \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left[a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'} + b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}, b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right] = \sum_{\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^3}E_{\mathbf{q}}\left(-\{b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\right) \\
&= -\sum_{\lambda'}\int\mathrm{d}^3q\,E_{\mathbf{q}}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{p}) = -E_{\mathbf{p}}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.252}
$$

设 $|E\rangle$ 是 $H$ 的本征态，本征值为 $E$，则

$$
H|E\rangle = E|E\rangle.
\tag{4.253}
$$

从而推出

$$
Ha_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle = (a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger H + E_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)|E\rangle = (E+E_{\mathbf{p}})a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle,
\tag{4.254}
$$

$$
Ha_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle = (a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}H - E_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda})|E\rangle = (E-E_{\mathbf{p}})a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle,
\tag{4.255}
$$

$$
Hb_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle = (b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger H + E_{\mathbf{p}}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)|E\rangle = (E+E_{\mathbf{p}})b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle,
\tag{4.256}
$$

$$
Hb_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle = (b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}H - E_{\mathbf{p}}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda})|E\rangle = (E-E_{\mathbf{p}})b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle.
\tag{4.257}
$$

可见，当 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle$ 和 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|E\rangle$ 不为零时，产生算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 和 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 的作用都是使能量本征值增加 $E_{\mathbf{p}}$；当 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle$ 和 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|E\rangle$ 不为零时，湮灭算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 和 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 的作用都是使能量本征值减少 $E_{\mathbf{p}}$。

根据 (1.192) 式，Dirac 旋量场的总动量算符为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{P} &= -\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\pi\nabla\psi = \int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\psi^\dagger(-\mathrm{i}\nabla)\psi \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right] \\
&\qquad\qquad\times\left[\mathbf{q}\,u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}q\cdot x} - \mathbf{q}\,v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}q\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q\,\mathbf{q}}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x}\right. \\
&\qquad - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p-q)\cdot x} - u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x} \\
&\qquad\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p+q)\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3q\,\mathbf{q}}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{q}}}}\left\{\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right.\right. \\
&\qquad\left.\left. - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right]\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + \delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{q})\left[-u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{q},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right.\right. \\
&\qquad\left.\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{q},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{q}})t}\right]\right\} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{\mathbf{p}}{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t} - v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t}\right] \\
&= \sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{\mathbf{p}}{2}\left(2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} - 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\mathbf{p}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right) = \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\mathbf{p}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} + b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right) - 2\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{0})\int\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathbf{p} \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\mathbf{p}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} + b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.258}
$$

倒数第四步用到正交归一关系 (4.201)，倒数第二步用到反对易关系 (4.247)。总动量是所有动量模式所有螺旋度所有粒子贡献的动量之和。

### 4.5.3 U(1) 整体对称性

类似于复标量场，Dirac 旋量场也具有 U(1) 整体对称性。对 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 作 U(1) 整体变换

$$
\psi'(x) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}q\theta}\psi(x),
\tag{4.259}
$$

则 $\psi^\dagger(x)$ 和 $\bar{\psi}(x)$ 的相应变换为

$$
[\psi^\dagger(x)]' = [\psi'(x)]^\dagger = \psi^\dagger(x)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}q\theta},\quad [\bar{\psi}(x)]' = \bar{\psi}'(x) = [\psi'(x)]^\dagger\gamma^0 = \bar{\psi}(x)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}q\theta}.
\tag{4.260}
$$

在此变换下，拉氏量 (4.103) 不变，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}'(x) &= \bar{\psi}'(x)(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi'(x) = \bar{\psi}(x)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}q\theta}(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}q\theta}\psi(x) \\
&= \bar{\psi}(x)(\mathrm{i}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi(x) = \mathcal{L}(x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.261}
$$

于是，跟复标量场一样，Dirac 旋量场具有相位任意性，相差一个任意整体相位因子 $\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\varphi}$ 的 Dirac 旋量场描述相同的物理。容易验证，4.3 节中列举的旋量双线性型都在这种 U(1) 整体变换下不变。因此，用这些旋量双线性型构造的拉氏量都具有 U(1) 整体对称性。

U(1) 整体变换的无穷小形式为

$$
\psi'(x) = \psi(x) + \mathrm{i}q\theta\psi(x).
\tag{4.262}
$$

由于 $\delta x^\mu = 0$，由 (1.166) 式得

$$
\delta\bar{\psi} = \delta\psi = \mathrm{i}q\theta\psi.
\tag{4.263}
$$

按照 (1.171) 式，相应的 Noether 守恒流为

$$
j^\mu = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_\mu\psi)}\delta\psi = \mathrm{i}\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu(\mathrm{i}q\theta\psi) = -q\theta\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\psi.
\tag{4.264}
$$

扔掉无穷小参数 $-\theta$，定义

$$
J^\mu \equiv q\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\psi,
\tag{4.265}
$$

则 Noether 定理给出

$$
\partial_\mu J^\mu = 0.
\tag{4.266}
$$

相应的守恒荷为

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \int\mathrm{d}^3x\,J^0 = q\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\bar{\psi}\gamma^0\psi = q\int\mathrm{d}^3x\,\psi^\dagger\psi \\
&= q\sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3k}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{k}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right] \\
&\qquad\qquad\times\left[u(\mathbf{k},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}k\cdot x} + v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}k\cdot x}\right] \\
&= q\sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3x\,\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3k}{(2\pi)^6\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{k}}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{k},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p-k)\cdot x}\right. \\
&\qquad + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p-k)\cdot x} + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(p+k)\cdot x} \\
&\qquad\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{k},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(p+k)\cdot x}\right] \\
&= q\sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p\,\mathrm{d}^3k}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}}E_{\mathbf{k}}}}\left\{\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{k})\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{k},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{k}})t}\right.\right. \\
&\qquad\left.\left. + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}-E_{\mathbf{k}})t}\right]\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + \delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{k})\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{k}})t} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{k},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(E_{\mathbf{p}}+E_{\mathbf{k}})t}\right]\right\} \\
&= q\sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3 2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\left[u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right. \\
&\qquad\left. + u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)v(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t} + v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},\lambda)u(-\mathbf{p},\lambda')b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{-\mathbf{p},\lambda'}\mathrm{e}^{-2\mathrm{i}E_{\mathbf{p}}t}\right] \\
&= q\sum_{\lambda\lambda'}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3 2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\left(2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'} + 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda'}^\dagger\right) = q\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\left(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} + b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\right) \\
&= \sum_{\lambda=\pm}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\left(q\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} - q\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\right) + 2\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{0})\int\mathrm{d}^3p\,q.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.267}
$$

上式第二项是零点荷。从第一项的形式可以看出，由 $(a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 描述的粒子是正粒子，携带的荷为 $q$；由 $(b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 描述的粒子是反粒子，携带的荷为 $-q$。除去零点荷，总荷是所有动量模式所有螺旋度所有正反粒子贡献的荷之和。

### 4.5.4 粒子态

对于自由的 Dirac 旋量场，真空态 $|0\rangle$ 定义为被任意 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 和任意 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 湮灭的态，

$$
a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|0\rangle = b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|0\rangle = 0,
\tag{4.268}
$$

满足

$$
\langle 0|0\rangle = 1,\quad H|0\rangle = E_{\text{vac}}|0\rangle,\quad E_{\text{vac}} = -2\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{0})\int\mathrm{d}^3p\,E_{\mathbf{p}}.
\tag{4.269}
$$

动量为 $\mathbf{p}$、螺旋度为 $\lambda$ 的单个正粒子态和单个反粒子态分别定义为

$$
|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle \equiv \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle,\quad |\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle \equiv \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle.
\tag{4.270}
$$

单粒子态的内积关系是

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\mathbf{q}^+,\lambda'|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle &= \sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{q}}E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,\langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = \sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{q}}E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,\langle 0|[(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) - a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}]|0\rangle \\
&= 2E_{\mathbf{p}}(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}),
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.271}
$$

$$
\langle\mathbf{q}^-,\lambda'|\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle = \sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{q}}E_{\mathbf{p}}}\,\langle 0|b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}).
\tag{4.272}
$$

根据 (4.254) 和 (4.256) 式，有

$$
H|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle = (E_{\text{vac}}+E_{\mathbf{p}})|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle,\quad H|\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle = (E_{\text{vac}}+E_{\mathbf{p}})|\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle.
\tag{4.273}
$$

可见，$|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle$ 和 $|\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle$ 都比真空态多了一份能量 $E_{\mathbf{p}} = \sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2+m^2}$。

将 $\psi(x)$ 的平面波解 (4.217) 代入 (4.68) 式左边，得

$$
[\psi(x),\mathbf{J}] = \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left\{u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},\mathbf{J}]\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,\mathbf{J}]\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right\},
\tag{4.274}
$$

代入右边，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathbf{L}+\mathbf{S})\psi(x) &= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}(-\mathrm{i}\mathbf{x}\times\nabla + \mathbf{S})\left[u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right] \\
&= \int\frac{\mathrm{d}^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{p}}}}\sum_{\lambda=\pm}\left[(\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}p\cdot x} + (-\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}p\cdot x}\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.275}
$$

两相比较，对于动量模式 $\mathbf{p}$ 和螺旋度 $\lambda$，有

$$
u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},\mathbf{J}] = (\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},\quad v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,\mathbf{J}] = (-\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger.
\tag{4.276}
$$

根据 (4.178) 和 (4.194) 式，$u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 分别是本征值为 $\lambda$ 和 $-\lambda$ 的螺旋度本征态，因而

$$
u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}] = 2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot(\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} = (2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S})u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda} = \lambda\,u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},
\tag{4.277}
$$

$$
v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)[b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}] = 2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot(-\mathbf{x}\times\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{S})v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger = (2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{S})v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger = -\lambda\,v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger.
\tag{4.278}
$$

故

$$
[a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}] = \lambda\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},\quad [b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}] = -\lambda\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger.
\tag{4.279}
$$

由于 $\mathbf{J}$ 是厄米算符，对第一式取厄米共轭得

$$
\lambda\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger = [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}]^\dagger = (2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J})a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger - a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}) = [2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger].
\tag{4.280}
$$

于是

$$
[2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J},a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger] = \lambda\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger,\quad [2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger] = \lambda\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger.
\tag{4.281}
$$

$\mathbf{J}$ 是总角动量算符，真空态 $|0\rangle$ 满足 $\mathbf{J}|0\rangle = \mathbf{0}$，由此得到

$$
(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J})a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = [a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}) + \lambda\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger]|0\rangle = \lambda\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle,
\tag{4.282}
$$

$$
(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J})b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = [b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}) + \lambda\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger]|0\rangle = \lambda\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle.
\tag{4.283}
$$

自由的单粒子态没有轨道角动量，而 $2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J}$ 相当于归一化的螺旋度算符。因此，上面两式说明 $|\mathbf{p}^+,\lambda\rangle$ 和 $|\mathbf{p}^-,\lambda\rangle$ 都是螺旋度本征态，本征值为 $\lambda$：

$$
(2\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\mathbf{J})|\mathbf{p}^\pm,\lambda\rangle = \lambda\,|\mathbf{p}^\pm,\lambda\rangle.
\tag{4.284}
$$

这正是我们所期望的。

以上讨论表明，产生算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 的作用是产生一个动量为 $\mathbf{p}$、螺旋度为 $\lambda$ 的正粒子，另一个产生算符 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 的作用是产生一个动量为 $\mathbf{p}$、螺旋度为 $\lambda$ 的反粒子。正粒子和反粒子具有相同的质量 $m$。

在 (4.189) 式中，我们选择让 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 正比于 $\xi_{-\lambda}(\mathbf{p})$，使得 $v(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 的螺旋度本征值为 $-\lambda$，从而得到 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle$ 的螺旋度本征值为 $\lambda$ 的结果。如果我们选择让 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 正比于 $\xi_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$，依照上述推导，$b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle$ 的螺旋度本征值就会变成 $-\lambda$；也就是说，$(b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda},b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger)$ 将描述螺旋度为 $-\lambda$ 的反粒子。这不符合我们的记号，因此，我们将 $\tilde{f}_\lambda(\mathbf{p})$ 取为 (4.189) 式的形式。

由反对易关系 (4.247)，有

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|\mathbf{q}^+,\lambda'\rangle &= \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}\,a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger|0\rangle = \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}\,[(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) - a_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}]|0\rangle \\
&= \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})|0\rangle,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.285}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}|\mathbf{q}^-,\lambda'\rangle &= \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}\,b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger|0\rangle = \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}\,[(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q}) - b_{\mathbf{q},\lambda'}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}]|0\rangle \\
&= \sqrt{2E_{\mathbf{q}}}(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})|0\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.286}
$$

可以看出，湮灭算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 的作用是减少一个动量为 $\mathbf{p}$、螺旋度为 $\lambda$ 的正粒子，湮灭算符 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}$ 的作用是减少一个动量为 $\mathbf{p}$、螺旋度为 $\lambda$ 的反粒子。

将包含 2 个正粒子和 2 个反粒子的态记为

$$
|\mathbf{p}_1^+,\lambda_1;\mathbf{p}_2^+,\lambda_2;\mathbf{p}_3^-,\lambda_3;\mathbf{p}_4^-,\lambda_4\rangle \equiv \sqrt{16E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}E_{\mathbf{p}_3}E_{\mathbf{p}_4}}\,a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p}_3,\lambda_3}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p}_4,\lambda_4}^\dagger|0\rangle.
\tag{4.287}
$$

多次利用反对易关系 (4.247)，调换产生算符的位置，可得

$$
a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p}_3,\lambda_3}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p}_4,\lambda_4}^\dagger|0\rangle = -b_{\mathbf{p}_4,\lambda_4}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p}_3,\lambda_3}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger|0\rangle,
\tag{4.288}
$$

负号源自奇数次反对易。从而

$$
|\mathbf{p}_4^-,\lambda_4;\mathbf{p}_2^+,\lambda_2;\mathbf{p}_3^-,\lambda_3;\mathbf{p}_1^+,\lambda_1\rangle = -|\mathbf{p}_1^+,\lambda_1;\mathbf{p}_2^+,\lambda_2;\mathbf{p}_3^-,\lambda_3;\mathbf{p}_4^-,\lambda_4\rangle,
\tag{4.289}
$$

即交换第 1 和第 4 个粒子得到的态与原来的态相差一个负号。同理，交换其中任意两个粒子，也会出现一个负号。

对于包含多个全同粒子的态，交换任意两个全同粒子，需要对产生算符进行奇数次反对易，得到的态与原来的态相差一个负号，也就是说，态对全同粒子交换是反对称的。这说明 Dirac 旋量场描述的粒子是一种费米子，称为 **Dirac 费米子**，服从 Fermi-Dirac 统计 [19, 20]。得到这个结论的关键在于两个产生算符反对易。对于两个相同的产生算符 $a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$ 或 $b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger$，反对易关系导致

$$
a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = -a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle,\quad b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = -b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle,
\tag{4.290}
$$

故

$$
a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = 0,\quad b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger b_{\mathbf{p},\lambda}^\dagger|0\rangle = 0.
\tag{4.291}
$$

这说明在没有其它自由度的情况下，不存在动量和螺旋度都相同的两个正费米子或两个反费米子组成的态，这符合 **Pauli 不相容原理** [21]。

在第 2 章和第 3 章中，我们分别讨论了自旋为 0 的标量场和自旋为 1 的矢量场，合适的处理方式是通过对易关系对它们进行量子化，因而它们都描述玻色子。另一方面，在本章中，我们需要采用反对易关系才能对自旋为 1/2 的旋量场进行合适的量子化，因而旋量场描述的粒子是费米子。实际上，这样的状况是普遍的，存在**自旋—统计定理** [22, 23]：

> 整数自旋的物理场必须用对易关系进行量子化，对应的粒子是玻色子；半整数自旋的物理场必须用反对易关系进行量子化，对应的粒子是费米子。

可以从多个角度证明这个定理必须成立。4.5.1 和 4.5.2 小节的讨论说明哈密顿量的正定性要求它成立。此外，也可以从交换全同粒子的路径依赖性、散射矩阵的 Lorentz 不变性或因果性的角度加以证明[^1]。

[^1]: 详细讨论见参考书【1】第 12 章。

将两个正费米子组成的双粒子态记为

$$
|\mathbf{p}_1^+,\lambda_1;\mathbf{p}_2^+,\lambda_2\rangle \equiv \sqrt{4E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}}\,a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger|0\rangle,
\tag{4.292}
$$

则双粒子态的内积关系是

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\mathbf{q}_1^+,\lambda_1';\mathbf{q}_2^+,\lambda_2'|\mathbf{p}_1^+,\lambda_1;\mathbf{p}_2^+,\lambda_2\rangle &= \sqrt{16E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}E_{\mathbf{q}_1}E_{\mathbf{q}_2}}\,\langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q}_2,\lambda_2'}a_{\mathbf{q}_1,\lambda_1'}a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger|0\rangle \\
&= \sqrt{16E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}E_{\mathbf{q}_1}E_{\mathbf{q}_2}}\left[(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_1'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_1)\langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q}_2,\lambda_2'}a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger|0\rangle\right. \\
&\qquad\left. - \langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q}_2,\lambda_2'}a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{q}_1,\lambda_1'}a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger|0\rangle\right] \\
&= \sqrt{16E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}E_{\mathbf{q}_1}E_{\mathbf{q}_2}}\left[(2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_1'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_1)\langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q}_2,\lambda_2'}a_{\mathbf{p}_2,\lambda_2}^\dagger|0\rangle\right. \\
&\qquad\left. - (2\pi)^3\delta_{\lambda_2\lambda_1'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{q}_1)\langle 0|a_{\mathbf{q}_2,\lambda_2'}a_{\mathbf{p}_1,\lambda_1}^\dagger|0\rangle\right] \\
&= \sqrt{16E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}E_{\mathbf{q}_1}E_{\mathbf{q}_2}}\left[(2\pi)^6\delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_1'}\delta_{\lambda_2\lambda_2'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_1)\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{q}_2)\right. \\
&\qquad\left. - (2\pi)^6\delta_{\lambda_2\lambda_1'}\delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_2'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{q}_1)\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_2)\right] \\
&= 4E_{\mathbf{p}_1}E_{\mathbf{p}_2}(2\pi)^6\left[\delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_1'}\delta_{\lambda_2\lambda_2'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_1)\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{q}_2)\right. \\
&\qquad\left. - \delta_{\lambda_1\lambda_2'}\delta_{\lambda_2\lambda_1'}\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{q}_2)\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{q}_1)\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.293}
$$

上式最后两行方括号中第二项前面有一个负号，由产生湮灭算符的反对易关系引起。这是双费米子态内积关系与双玻色子态内积关系 (2.161) 在形式上的不同之处。

## 习题

4.1 证明下列等式。

(a) $\gamma^\mu\not{p} = 2p^\mu - \not{p}\gamma^\mu$.

(b) $\not{p}\not{p} = p^2$.

(c) $\{\not{p}\not{k}\not{q},\gamma^\mu\} = 2p^\mu\not{k}\not{q} - 2k^\mu\not{p}\not{q} + 2q^\mu\not{p}\not{k}$.

(d) $\gamma^\mu\gamma_\mu = 4$.

(e) $\sigma^{\mu\nu}\sigma_{\mu\nu} = 12$.

(f) $\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\sigma^{\rho\sigma} = -24\mathrm{i}\gamma^5$.

(g) $\gamma_\mu\gamma^5 = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{6}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma$.

(h) $[\gamma_\mu,\gamma_\nu]\gamma^5 = \mathrm{i}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma$.

(i) $\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\sigma^{\rho\sigma} = -2\mathrm{i}\sigma_{\mu\nu}\gamma^5$.

4.2 设自由 Dirac 旋量场 $\psi(x)$ 的拉氏量为

$$
\mathcal{L} = \frac{\mathrm{i}}{2}\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\overleftrightarrow{\partial}_\mu\psi - m\bar{\psi}\psi,
\tag{4.294}
$$

证明由 Euler-Lagrange 方程 (1.142) 推出的经典运动方程也是 Dirac 方程 (4.107)。

4.3 对于平面波旋量系数 $u(\mathbf{p},\lambda)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{k},\lambda')$，证明下列等式。

(a) $(\bar{u}\gamma^\mu v)^* = \bar{v}\gamma^\mu u$.

(b) $(\bar{u}\gamma^5 v)^* = -\bar{v}\gamma^5 u$.

(c) $(\bar{u}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 v)^* = \bar{v}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5 u$.

(d) $(\bar{u}\sigma^{\mu\nu}v)^* = \bar{v}\sigma^{\mu\nu}u$.

(e) $(\bar{u}\gamma^5\sigma^{\mu\nu}v)^* = -\bar{v}\gamma^5\sigma^{\mu\nu}u$.

4.4 证明 **Gordon** 恒等式

$$
\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\gamma^\mu u(\mathbf{k},\lambda') = \bar{u}(\mathbf{p},\lambda)\left(\frac{p^\mu+k^\mu}{2m} + \frac{\mathrm{i}\sigma^{\mu\nu}q_\nu}{2m}\right)u(\mathbf{k},\lambda'),
\tag{4.295}
$$

其中 $q^\mu \equiv p^\mu - k^\mu$。

4.5 在球坐标系中，动量表达为 $\mathbf{p} = |\mathbf{p}|\hat{\mathbf{p}} = |\mathbf{p}|(s_\theta c_\phi,s_\theta s_\phi,c_\theta)$，其中 $s_\theta \equiv \sin\theta$，$c_\theta \equiv \cos\theta$。

(a) 推出

$$
\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \begin{pmatrix} c_\theta & \mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\phi}s_\theta \\ \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\phi}s_\theta & -c_\theta \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.296}
$$

(b) 推出螺旋态表达式

$$
\xi_+(\mathbf{p}) = \begin{pmatrix} c_{\theta/2} \\ \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\phi}s_{\theta/2} \end{pmatrix},\quad \xi_-(\mathbf{p}) = \begin{pmatrix} -\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\phi}s_{\theta/2} \\ c_{\theta/2} \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.297}
$$

(c) 根据以上两步结果验证 $(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_+(\mathbf{p}) = +\xi_+(\mathbf{p})$，$(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\xi_-(\mathbf{p}) = -\xi_-(\mathbf{p})$。

(d) 证明

$$
\exp(\mathrm{i}\alpha\,\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}) = \cos\alpha + \mathrm{i}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma})\sin\alpha.
\tag{4.298}
$$

4.6 在 **Dirac 表象**（也称为**标准表象**）中，$\gamma$ 矩阵表达为

$$
\gamma^0 = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1} & \\ & -\mathbf{1} \end{pmatrix},\quad \gamma^i = \begin{pmatrix} & \sigma^i \\ -\sigma^i & \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.299}
$$

(a) 验证它们满足反对易关系 (4.1)。

(b) 推出

$$
\gamma^5 = \begin{pmatrix} & \mathbf{1} \\ \mathbf{1} & \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.300}
$$

(c) 将 Dirac 表象中的平面波旋量系数表达为

$$
u(\mathbf{p},s) = \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+m}\begin{pmatrix} \zeta_s \\ \frac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mathbf{p}}{E_{\mathbf{p}}+m}\zeta_s \end{pmatrix},\quad v(\mathbf{p},s) = \sqrt{E_{\mathbf{p}}+m}\begin{pmatrix} \frac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mathbf{p}}{E_{\mathbf{p}}+m}\eta_s \\ \eta_s \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.301}
$$

$\zeta_s$ 不依赖于动量 $\mathbf{p}$，是某个固定方向上的二分量自旋本征态，本征值为 $s = \pm 1/2$；$\eta_s$ 是另一套类似的本征态，可以跟 $\zeta_s$ 不同；它们满足

$$
\zeta_s^\dagger\zeta_{s'} = \delta_{ss'},\quad \sum_{s=\pm 1/2}\zeta_s\zeta_s^\dagger = \mathbf{1},
\tag{4.302}
$$

$$
\eta_s^\dagger\eta_{s'} = \delta_{ss'},\quad \sum_{s=\pm 1/2}\eta_s\eta_s^\dagger = \mathbf{1}.
\tag{4.303}
$$

证明 $u(\mathbf{p},s)$ 和 $v(\mathbf{p},s)$ 满足运动方程

$$
(\not{p}-m)u(\mathbf{p},s) = 0,\quad (\not{p}+m)v(\mathbf{p},s) = 0,
\tag{4.304}
$$

正交归一关系

$$
u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},s)u(\mathbf{p},s') = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{ss'},\quad v^\dagger(\mathbf{p},s)v(\mathbf{p},s') = 2E_{\mathbf{p}}\delta_{ss'},\quad u^\dagger(\mathbf{p},s)v(-\mathbf{p},s') = 0,
\tag{4.305}
$$

和自旋求和关系

$$
\sum_{s=\pm 1/2}u(\mathbf{p},s)\bar{u}(\mathbf{p},s) = \not{p}+m,\quad \sum_{s=\pm 1/2}v(\mathbf{p},s)\bar{v}(\mathbf{p},s) = \not{p}-m.
\tag{4.306}
$$

4.7 将 Weyl 表象中的 $\gamma$ 矩阵 (4.69) 记为 $\gamma_{\mathrm{W}}^\mu$，Dirac 表象中的 $\gamma$ 矩阵 (4.299) 记为 $\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}^\mu$，寻找幺正矩阵 $U$，使得 $\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}^\mu = U^\dagger\gamma_{\mathrm{W}}^\mu U$。
